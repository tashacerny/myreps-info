summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill uses the Congressional Review Act (CRA) to disapprove a specific Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) rule. The rule in question addresses pollution control standards for nonroad engines in California and sets requirements for ocean-going vessels while they are docked at berths (at-berth operations). Under the CRA process, Congress can vote to reject a federal agency rule within a set timeframe after it is issued. If this disapproval passes both chambers and is signed by the President, the EPA rule would be invalidated and the agency would be prohibited from reissuing substantially similar rules without new congressional authorization.  The rule affects California's authority to set its own vehicle and engine emission standards (a power granted under the Clean Air Act), vessel operators using California ports, and potentially the EPA's regulatory authority in this area. The bill would determine whether this particular pollution control standard takes effect or is blocked from implementation.  Key to understanding this bill's impact is that it represents a legislative veto of an executive agency action. A \"yes\" vote disapproves the EPA rule; a \"no\" vote allows it to stand."
