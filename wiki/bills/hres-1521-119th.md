summary: "# Bill Summary: American Cleaning Institute 100th Anniversary Recognition  This bill is a commemorative measure that honors the 100th anniversary of the American Cleaning Institute (ACI), a trade organization representing manufacturers and distributors of cleaning products. The bill recognizes the contributions of the cleaning products industry to public health, safety, and the U.S. economy. As a resolution rather than legislation creating new law, this bill serves to formally acknowledge this milestone and the industry's role in American society.  The bill affects members and stakeholders of the cleaning products industry, including manufacturers, distributors, and related businesses represented by the ACI. More broadly, it acknowledges the impact of the cleaning industry on the general public, as cleaning products are widely used in households, businesses, healthcare facilities, and other settings across the country. The measure has no direct regulatory or budgetary implications.  Key provisions of the bill include formal recognition of the ACI's 100-year history and acknowledgment of the industry's contributions to health and safety—particularly relevant given the essential role cleaning products played during public health emergencies. The bill underscores the economic significance of an industry that employs workers in manufacturing, distribution, and related sectors throughout the United States."
