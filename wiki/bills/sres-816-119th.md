summary: "# Bill Summary  This resolution commemorates the 35th anniversary of independence for five Central Asian nations: Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan, and Uzbekistan. These countries gained independence following the dissolution of the Soviet Union in 1991. The resolution acknowledges this historical milestone and expresses the intent to recognize the significance of this period in the region's history.  The resolution also emphasizes the importance of strengthening and developing the United States' diplomatic and bilateral relationships with these five Central Asian countries. It highlights the growing nature of U.S. engagement with the region and signals congressional interest in continued cooperation. The resolution does not create new law or appropriations but serves as a formal statement of Congress regarding these nations and U.S. policy priorities in Central Asia.  This resolution affects U.S. foreign policy messaging and diplomatic relations with Central Asia. It is primarily symbolic in nature, intended to affirm congressional support for engagement with Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan, and Uzbekistan during an important anniversary year for these nations."
