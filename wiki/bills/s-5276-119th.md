summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill proposes to modify the Robert T. Stafford Disaster Relief and Emergency Assistance Act, which is the primary federal law governing disaster assistance programs. Specifically, the bill would change the time period rules for determining which wages qualify for federal reimbursement when workers repair, restore, or replace facilities damaged by disasters. The change applies to both regular base wages and overtime wages paid to workers performing this recovery work.  The bill affects employers and workers involved in disaster recovery efforts, as well as federal and state agencies that manage disaster assistance funding. When a disaster occurs and federal assistance is approved, the government reimburses eligible costs for repairing public and private facilities. By adjusting the eligibility period for wage reimbursement, the bill would alter which labor costs qualify for federal coverage during the recovery process.  While the specific details of the time period adjustment are not available from the title alone, such changes typically address practical implementation issues—for example, extending or narrowing the window during which wages must be incurred to be eligible for reimbursement, or clarifying how wage calculations should be handled during the recovery phase. This would directly impact how much federal funding is available for disaster reconstruction efforts and which workers' compensation qualifies for government support."
