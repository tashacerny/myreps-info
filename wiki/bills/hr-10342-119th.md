The legislation proposes to extend these financial assistance programs to businesses involved in commercial fishing and mariculture (farm-raising of aquatic organisms), making them newly eligible to apply for USDA loans and grants.  The bill would affect fishing operations and mariculture farms seeking capital for business operations, equipment purchases, land acquisition, or other development needs. It would also impact USDA agencies that administer rural lending programs, which would need to implement eligibility changes and potentially develop new procedures to serve these industries.  Key provisions would likely include establishing eligibility criteria for fishing and mariculture businesses, defining which types of operations qualify for assistance, and determining any special conditions or requirements specific to these industries.
