summary: "# Bill Summary: Botanical Drug Market Exclusivity  This bill would amend the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act to create a period of market exclusivity for botanical drugs—medications derived from plants or plant materials. Market exclusivity prevents the FDA from approving competing generic or biosimilar versions of a drug for a specified time period, even if those versions meet regulatory standards. This provision would apply to botanical drug products that receive FDA approval, giving their manufacturers a defined period during which they would have exclusive rights to market those products.  The bill would primarily affect pharmaceutical manufacturers that develop botanical drugs, as well as generic drug manufacturers who would be restricted from entering the market during the exclusivity period. Patients and consumers could experience effects through drug pricing, as market exclusivity typically allows higher prices before generic competition arrives. The FDA would also be involved in implementing and administering the exclusivity provision.  Beyond establishing botanical drug exclusivity, the bill's title indicates it contains additional provisions (\"and for other purposes\"), though specific details are not provided in the available information. The full scope and any other modifications to the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act would require review of the complete legislative text."
