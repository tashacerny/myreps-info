summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill directs the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to include two specific counties in New Mexico within a particular Veterans Integrated Service Network (VISN). VISNs are regional networks that organize and deliver VA healthcare services across the United States. The bill would change which VISN provides services to these two New Mexico counties, likely affecting how veterans in those counties access VA medical care, benefits processing, and other services.  The bill primarily affects veterans residing in the two named New Mexico counties, who would receive services through a different regional VA network than they currently do. It may also affect VA operations by requiring the reorganization of service delivery and resource allocation in the affected region.  The key provision is the directive to the VA Secretary to administratively reassign these two counties to the specified VISN. The bill does not appear to create new programs or significantly alter VA operations beyond this geographic realignment of service responsibility."
