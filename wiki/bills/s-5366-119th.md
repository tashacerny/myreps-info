summary: "# Summary of Low-Income Housing Tax Credit Carryback Bill  This bill would modify the Internal Revenue Code to allow taxpayers to carry back low-income housing tax credit (LIHTC) amounts for up to five years. Currently, these credits can only be carried forward to future tax years. A carryback provision would permit taxpayers to apply unused credits against taxes owed in prior years, potentially resulting in refunds or reduced taxes paid in those earlier periods.  The bill would primarily affect entities involved in low-income housing development and financing, including real estate developers, investors, and housing organizations that claim the LIHTC. These credits are generally used to offset federal income taxes owed by investors in affordable housing projects. The five-year carryback window would give these taxpayers greater flexibility in managing their tax liabilities across multiple years.  Key provisions would include defining the carryback period (five years) and specifying the mechanics of how credits could be applied to prior tax returns. The change aims to improve the financial incentives for investment in low-income housing projects by allowing taxpayers to recover taxes paid in earlier years when they have excess credits."
