summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill proposes to amend federal tax law to eliminate certain fees that taxpayers must pay when setting up installment agreements with the Internal Revenue Service (IRS). An installment agreement allows taxpayers who cannot pay their full tax bill at once to pay their taxes over time in scheduled monthly payments. Currently, the IRS charges fees to establish and maintain these agreements. This bill would remove those fees for specified groups of taxpayers.  The bill would directly affect individuals who owe federal income taxes and choose to pay through an installment plan rather than in full. The specific groups eligible for fee elimination are not detailed in the title alone, but such provisions typically target lower-income taxpayers or those experiencing financial hardship. By removing these fees, the bill aims to reduce the cost burden of paying taxes in installments.  The key provision is the elimination of installment agreement fees for the defined population. The practical effect would be to lower the total cost of using an IRS payment plan for affected taxpayers, making installment payments a less expensive option and potentially reducing barriers to compliance for those unable to pay their tax liability immediately."
