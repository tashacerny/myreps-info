summary: "# Summary: Wildland Firefighters Congressional Gold Medal Act  This bill proposes to award the Congressional Gold Medal to wildland firefighters in recognition of their service and contributions. The Congressional Gold Medal is one of the highest civilian honors that Congress can bestow and is typically awarded to individuals or groups for outstanding achievements or service to the United States. If enacted, this legislation would direct the U.S. Mint to strike and present a gold medal honoring wildland firefighters.  The bill recognizes wildland firefighters—personnel who fight fires in forests, grasslands, and other unstructured terrain—as a group deserving of this honor. Wildland firefighters include both federal employees (such as those with the U.S. Forest Service and Bureau of Land Management) and state, local, and contracted personnel who battle large-scale fires, often in remote and dangerous conditions. The award would acknowledge the risks these workers face and their role in protecting public lands, property, and communities.  Key provisions would include the authorization and minting of the Congressional Gold Medal, and potentially the establishment of arrangements for its display or presentation. The bill may also include technical specifications for the medal's design and any associated ceremonies or commemorative events."
