The bill would affect data center operators and owners, technology companies that own or operate these facilities, and potentially regulatory agencies tasked with collecting and publishing this information. It could also impact investors, policymakers, and the public who seek information about how much water and energy these increasingly important facilities consume.  Key provisions would likely include specific reporting requirements (such as what metrics must be tracked and how often), standards for measuring water and energy use, timelines for compliance, and mechanisms for making this information available—potentially through a public database or regular disclosures.
