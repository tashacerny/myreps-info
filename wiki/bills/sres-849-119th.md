summary: "# Summary of Patriot Week Resolution  This resolution expresses Congressional support for designating the week of September 11–17, 2026, as \"Patriot Week.\" The designation would create an official observance period during the week that includes September 11, the anniversary of the 2001 terrorist attacks. The resolution itself is a symbolic measure that does not create new law or mandate any specific government actions.  The resolution would affect the general public by designating a week for remembrance and patriotic observance. It encourages individuals, organizations, and communities to participate in activities honoring those affected by September 11 and promoting civic engagement during that week. Government agencies and institutions may use the designation to coordinate official commemorative activities, though the resolution does not require them to do so.  The key provision is the official designation of the specific week as \"Patriot Week.\" Resolutions of this type typically encourage voluntary participation and awareness rather than imposing requirements or restrictions on any group."
