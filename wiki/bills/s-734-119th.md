summary: "# Summary: Edward J. Dwight, Jr. Congressional Gold Medal Act of 2025  This bill authorizes the awarding of a Congressional Gold Medal to Edward J. Dwight, Jr., one of the nation's highest civilian honors. The medal recognizes exceptional achievement and service to the United States. Based on the bill title, this legislation specifically honors Dwight's accomplishments and contributions.  Edward J. Dwight, Jr. is a pioneering aviator and entrepreneur. He was selected as part of the Air Force's astronaut training program in the early 1960s and remains a significant figure in American aviation history. The bill reflects recognition of his career achievements and broader impact.  The Congressional Gold Medal is a symbolic honor with no direct financial or programmatic effects. Passage of this bill would authorize the medal's design, striking, and presentation to Dwight or his designee. The legislation affects no other individuals or programs and carries no appropriations requirement beyond the minimal costs of medal production and a possible ceremony."
