summary: "# Summary of Congressional Disapproval Resolution  This resolution uses the Congressional Review Act (CRA) to overturn a specific Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) rule. The rule in question grants California a waiver that allows the state to set its own greenhouse gas emission standards for new motor vehicles starting with 2009 model year vehicles, rather than being subject to federal standards. Under the Clean Air Act, California has historically been permitted to seek waivers from federal preemption to establish stricter pollution controls than the national standard.  The resolution would disapprove of the EPA's decision to grant this waiver. If passed by both chambers of Congress and signed by the President (or passed over a presidential veto), the rule would be repealed and cannot be reissued in substantially the same form. This would prevent California from implementing its own greenhouse gas standards and would require the state to follow federal emission standards instead.  **Who it affects:** California; automakers; potentially other states that follow California's emission standards under Clean Air Act provisions; and consumers purchasing vehicles in those states. The practical effect would be to standardize vehicle emission requirements nationally rather than allowing California to maintain separate standards."
