summary: "# Summary of the Strategic Grazing to Reduce Risk of Wildfire Act  **What the Bill Proposes**  This bill authorizes the use of livestock grazing as a land management tool to reduce wildfire risk on federal and potentially state lands. The legislation would establish or expand programs that deploy cattle, sheep, goats, and other grazing animals to remove dried vegetation, dead plants, and undergrowth that can fuel large wildfires. The bill aims to integrate grazing into wildfire prevention strategies alongside other methods like prescribed burns and mechanical clearing.  **Who It Affects**  The bill impacts federal land management agencies (such as the Forest Service and Bureau of Land Management), ranchers and livestock operators, and communities in fire-prone regions. Ranchers may gain access to additional grazing opportunities on public lands, while agencies would receive authorization and potentially funding to coordinate grazing projects. Property owners and residents in wildfire-risk areas could benefit from reduced fuel loads.  **Key Provisions**  The legislation likely includes provisions that encourage or subsidize grazing operations on federal lands for wildfire mitigation purposes, streamline permitting processes for temporary or strategic grazing, fund pilot programs, and possibly establish liability protections or cost-sharing arrangements between agencies and ranchers. The bill may also require coordination between land management agencies and specify which lands are suitable for grazing-based fuel reduction."
