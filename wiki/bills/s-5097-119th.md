summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill proposes to amend the Immigration and Nationality Act by creating a temporary pause on issuing H-1B visas. H-1B visas are used to employ foreign workers in specialty occupations in the United States. The pause would remain in effect until the government implements certain limitations on how many H-1B visas can be issued and under what conditions.  The bill would affect employers who seek to hire foreign workers for specialty occupations, as well as prospective H-1B visa applicants. During the pause period, no new H-1B visas could be issued, though the bill does not specify whether existing visa holders or visa extensions would be affected.  Key provisions would include halting new H-1B visa issuances and requiring the government to establish and implement unspecified limitations before visa issuance resumes. The exact nature of these limitations is not detailed in the title alone. This measure could impact industries that rely on H-1B workers, including technology, healthcare, and engineering sectors."
