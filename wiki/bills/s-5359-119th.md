The bill affects federal land management in the county and would shift administrative responsibility for the identified parcels from the U.S. Department of Agriculture's Forest Service to local county control.  The key provision is the conveyance (transfer) of the designated National Forest land to Pennington County. This action would remove the affected acreage from the federal National Forest System, allowing the county to manage, use, or develop the land according to local priorities. The bill likely specifies the exact parcels to be transferred and may include conditions or requirements related to the conveyance, though these details are not available in the title alone.  This type of legislation primarily affects Pennington County (which includes the Black Hills region), the Forest Service's management scope, and potentially local stakeholders with interests in the affected land.
