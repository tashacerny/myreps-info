summary: "# Bill Summary  This joint resolution seeks to disapprove a specific Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) rule under the Congressional Review Act (CRA). The rule in question pertains to California's nonroad engine pollution control standards and commercial harbor craft regulations. Under the CRA process, Congress can vote to overturn federal agency regulations within a set timeframe after they are issued. If passed, this resolution would invalidate the EPA rule related to these California emissions standards.  The rule affects California's authority to set its own pollution control standards for nonroad engines and commercial harbor craft (such as tugboats and ferries), as well as any entities operating such equipment in or subject to California regulations. It also affects the EPA's decision regarding California's regulatory authority in this area. The specific provisions of the underlying EPA rule—which would be overturned if this resolution passes—are not detailed in the title alone and would require review of the full regulatory text.  This type of resolution requires a simple majority vote in both the House and Senate to pass. If successful, it would prevent the EPA rule from taking effect (or rescind it if already in effect) and generally prevents the EPA from issuing a substantially similar rule without new legislative authorization."
