summary: "# Summary: National Park Week Designation Resolution  This resolution designates the week of August 22–30, 2026, as \"National Park Week.\" The measure is a congressional resolution intended to recognize and promote awareness of the nation's park system during this specific week.  The resolution affects the general public and organizations involved in park management and promotion, including the National Park Service, state park agencies, and conservation groups. The designation has no direct regulatory or budgetary impact but serves as an official acknowledgment that may be used to encourage public visitation, educational activities, and celebrations related to parks during that period.  Key provisions include the official designation of the date range and would typically authorize the President to issue a proclamation recognizing National Park Week. This type of resolution is primarily symbolic in nature, intended to draw attention to parks and their recreational, historical, and environmental value without creating new laws or funding requirements."
