As a resolution rather than legislation, this action does not create new law or programs but instead expresses official support for recognizing and promoting this awareness effort during the designated month.  The resolution affects the general public by encouraging focus on substance use prevention during October 2025. It may prompt government agencies, public health organizations, schools, and community groups to organize educational activities and awareness campaigns during this period. The designation targets audiences interested in public health, addiction prevention, treatment resources, and substance use policy.  Key aspects of the resolution include its symbolic nature—establishing an official month of recognition—and its goal of promoting prevention-focused awareness rather than other approaches to substance use issues.
