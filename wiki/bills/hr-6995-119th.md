summary: "# Bill Summary: Jeremy Malone Post Office Designation  This bill proposes to officially designate the United States Postal Service facility located at 825 Highway 198 in Beaumont, Mississippi, with the name \"Jeremy Malone Post Office.\" The legislation is a commemorative measure that would rename the existing postal facility after Jeremy Malone.  The bill directly affects the postal facility in Beaumont and would result in a change to its official name. This type of designation does not alter the facility's operations, services, or functions—it is purely a naming honor. The change would affect signage, official correspondence, and postal service documentation related to that location. Residents and businesses in Beaumont who use this postal facility would reference it under its new official name."
