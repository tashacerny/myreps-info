summary: "# Bill Summary  This commemorative resolution marks the one-year anniversary of a violent incident that occurred on September 10, 2025, in Evergreen, Colorado. The bill formally recognizes and honors the individuals affected by this event, including those killed, injured, and traumatized, as well as the first responders who assisted at the scene.  The resolution expresses the legislative body's condolences to the families of victims and affected community members. It aims to acknowledge the impact of the tragedy on the Evergreen community and surrounding areas. As a commemorative measure, the bill does not create new law or establish programs; rather, it serves as an official statement of recognition and support during a period of remembrance."
