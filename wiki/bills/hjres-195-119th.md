summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill would use the Congressional Review Act (CRA) to reject a Department of Health and Human Services regulation titled \"Restoring Flexibility in the Child Care and Development Fund (CCDF).\" Under the CRA process, Congress can disapprove of federal agency rules within 60 legislative days of their submission. If passed and signed into law, this bill would nullify the HHS rule and prevent the agency from issuing substantially similar regulations without explicit congressional authorization.  The rule being disapproved concerns the Child Care and Development Fund, a federal program that provides grants to states to help low-income families afford child care services. The specific provisions of the HHS rule are not detailed in the title, but it appears to involve modifications to CCDF regulations to increase flexibility, likely in how states administer the program or implement requirements. The bill would affect HHS policy implementation, state child care administrators, and potentially families and child care providers who use or participate in the CCDF program.  The bill's practical effect depends on whether it passes both chambers and receives the President's signature. If enacted, it would block the HHS regulatory changes and potentially return rules to their previous form, unless HHS takes different regulatory action. Congress and stakeholders would need to review the full text of the underlying regulation to understand the specific policy areas being reversed."
