summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill proposes to amend federal law regarding military personnel standards by updating language in Title 10 of the United States Code and the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 1994 to use gender-neutral terminology and standards. The amendments would apply to requirements, qualifications, and regulations governing members of the Armed Forces, replacing gender-specific language with gender-neutral alternatives where applicable.  The bill affects all service members in the Armed Forces by standardizing the legal language that describes their duties, rights, and requirements. It also impacts military leadership and personnel management systems that must implement these clarified standards.  Key provisions include codifying gender-neutral language throughout relevant military statutes and clarifying that existing standards apply equally regardless of gender. The bill seeks to ensure consistency in how military law addresses all service members and to remove outdated gender-specific terminology while maintaining the substantive requirements and standards for military service. This is primarily a technical and clarifying measure focused on updating statutory language rather than proposing new policies or benefits."
