summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill would amend the Higher Education Act of 1965 to modify accreditation standards for colleges and universities. Specifically, it would require accrediting bodies to evaluate whether institutions have adopted admissions practices that do not give preferential treatment to applicants who are children or relatives of alumni or major donors. The bill effectively directs accreditors to assess institutions' policies on \"legacy preferences\" and donor-connected admissions advantages as part of their accreditation review process.  The bill affects accrediting agencies, institutions of higher education, and prospective students. It would establish a new criterion for institutional accreditation focused on eliminating or minimizing admissions advantages based on familial or financial connections to the institution. Additionally, the bill authorizes a feasibility study on data collection related to these admissions practices, suggesting the creation of mechanisms to track and report on institutions' legacy and donor preference policies.  The practical effect would be to incentivize or require colleges and universities to eliminate or restrict legacy and donor-preference admissions policies if they wish to maintain accreditation. This could broaden access to higher education by reducing advantages for applicants from wealthy or alumni-connected families, though it would also represent a significant change to admissions practices that many institutions currently employ."
