summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill expresses congressional condemnation of a terrorist attack on the Argentine Jewish Mutual Association (AMIA) Jewish Community Center in Buenos Aires that occurred in July 1994. The attack killed 85 people and injured many others, making it one of the deadliest terrorist attacks in Argentine history. The bill formally denounces this violence against the Jewish community center.  In addition to condemning the attack, the bill encourages accountability for those responsible. This means the bill calls for efforts to identify, prosecute, and hold accountable the individuals and organizations behind the bombing. The bill affects Argentina's government and any international bodies involved in investigating or prosecuting those responsible for the attack. The bill serves as a statement of U.S. congressional position on this historical incident and reaffirms support for accountability in terrorist cases targeting civilian populations."
