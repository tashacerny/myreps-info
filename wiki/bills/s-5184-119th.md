The program would establish and test the use of mobile vehicles or similar delivery methods to provide meals in areas where children might not otherwise have regular access to food assistance during school breaks. This addresses a gap in meal support that currently exists between the school year, when programs like the National School Lunch Program operate, and the summer when many children lose access to subsidized meals.  The pilot program would directly affect school districts, nonprofit organizations, and state education agencies that participate, as well as children in low-income areas who depend on meal assistance programs. It may also involve the U.S. Department of Agriculture, which administers the National School Lunch Act.
