summary: "# Summary: No Taxpayer Funds for Congressional First Class Flights Act  This bill would restrict the use of federal funds for first-class air travel by members of Congress. The legislation targets the practice of using taxpayer money to purchase premium cabin seats on commercial flights, proposing that such funds may only be used for economy-class tickets or other lower-cost travel options.  The bill affects members of the House and Senate who currently travel on official business using appropriated funds. Under this proposal, Congress members would still be able to fly first-class if they choose to do so, but would be required to cover the difference between the economy fare and the first-class fare with personal funds. Key provisions would likely establish clear rules defining which travel qualifies as official business and set procedures for reimbursement or cost-sharing arrangements."
