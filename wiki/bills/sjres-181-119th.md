summary: "# Bill Summary  This joint resolution directs the removal of U.S. Armed Forces from any hostilities involving Iran that lack congressional authorization. The resolution affirms that military operations require explicit congressional approval under the War Powers Resolution and the Constitution, and asserts that any ongoing or future military actions against Iran must be authorized by Congress before they can proceed. The measure would apply to all U.S. military personnel engaged in such hostilities.  The resolution affects the Department of Defense and any executive branch agencies directing military operations. It impacts service members who might be deployed in operations against Iran and establishes a legal requirement that such operations cease if not explicitly authorized by Congress. The resolution does not appear to modify existing military operations already approved by Congress, nor does it restrict defensive military actions.  The key provision directs the President to remove armed forces from Iran-related hostilities that lack congressional authorization. This reflects ongoing constitutional debate over war powers—specifically, whether the President may direct military action without prior congressional approval, or whether such authority belongs exclusively to Congress. The resolution would enforce the congressional authorization requirement through this directive."
