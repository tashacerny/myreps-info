summary: "# Summary of Asian American and Native American Pacific Islander-Serving Institutions Week Resolution  This resolution designates the week of September 22–28, 2025, as \"Asian American and Native American Pacific Islander-Serving Institutions Week.\" The measure expresses congressional support for recognizing and honoring colleges and universities that serve Asian American, Native American, and Pacific Islander student populations. It does not create new federal programs or funding but rather establishes an official designation to raise awareness during that specific week.  The resolution affects higher education institutions that have significant enrollment of Asian American, Native American, and Pacific Islander students, as well as the students themselves. It may also be of interest to education advocacy organizations, federal agencies involved in education policy, and policymakers concerned with supporting underrepresented or underserved communities in higher education.  The key provision is the designation of the week itself. Such resolutions typically serve a ceremonial or symbolic purpose—encouraging recognition and celebration of the designated institutions and communities—rather than directing substantive policy changes or appropriating funds."
