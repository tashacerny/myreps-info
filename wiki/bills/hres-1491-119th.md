summary: "# Summary: Black Breastfeeding Week Designation (2026)  This bill expresses support for designating the week of August 25–31, 2026, as \"Black Breastfeeding Week.\" The measure is a symbolic resolution intended to recognize and raise awareness about breastfeeding in Black communities. Such designations do not create new programs or funding, but rather officially acknowledge a specific observance period.  The resolution would primarily affect public awareness and recognition efforts. It may be promoted by health organizations, community groups, and government agencies focused on maternal and infant health, particularly those working with Black populations. The designation could be used to highlight relevant health information, community events, or educational initiatives during that week.  Key aspects include the specific dates (August 25–31, 2026) and the focus on Black communities. The bill does not establish legal requirements, allocate funds, or create new government programs—it functions as an official statement of support for the designated observance."
