summary: "# Bill Summary  **Purpose and Scope**  This bill would authorize the Director of the Bureau of Prisons to provide postage stamps to inmates who lack the financial means to purchase them. The measure addresses communication access for incarcerated individuals classified as indigent, allowing them to send mail without the ability to pay for postage.  **Key Provision and Affected Parties**  The primary provision grants the Bureau of Prisons Director discretionary authority to distribute postage stamps to eligible inmates. The policy would affect incarcerated individuals in the federal prison system who meet indigency criteria, as well as the Bureau of Prisons, which would bear the cost of the stamps. This could also indirectly affect inmates' family members and contacts who receive mail, as it enables communication from individuals who would otherwise be unable to correspond."
