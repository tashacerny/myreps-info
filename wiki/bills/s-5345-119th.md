summary: "# Bill Summary: Artificial Intelligence Functional Bills of Materials  **What the Bill Proposes**  This bill would require the delivery of \"artificial intelligence functional bills of materials\" (AI-FBoMs), a documentation system designed to provide transparency about how artificial intelligence systems are constructed and operate. The bill appears to establish requirements for disclosing the components, data sources, computational processes, and other technical elements that make up AI systems, similar to how traditional bills of materials list the components in manufactured products.  **Who It Affects**  The bill would likely affect organizations that develop, deploy, or use AI systems, as well as users and stakeholders who interact with these systems. This could include AI companies, software developers, businesses using AI tools, and potentially consumers or regulators seeking greater visibility into how AI systems function.  **Key Purpose**  The stated aim is to increase transparency and accountability in AI development and deployment by making the composition and functionality of AI systems more clearly documented and available. This type of documentation could help with understanding AI system capabilities, identifying potential risks, enabling better regulatory oversight, and supporting more informed decision-making about AI use.  ---  *Note: This summary is based on the bill title only.
