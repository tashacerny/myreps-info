summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill would authorize the Secretary of Health and Human Services to award grants for training community mental wellness workers. The grants would be administered through the Assistant Secretary for Mental Health and Substance Use, a division within HHS. The legislation aims to expand the mental health workforce by supporting education and training programs for individuals who work directly in their communities to provide mental health support and services.  The bill would affect mental health training organizations, educational institutions, and communities seeking to develop local mental health capacity. It would also impact individuals pursuing careers as community mental wellness workers—a category that typically includes peer specialists, community health workers, and other non-licensed mental health professionals who provide support at the local level.  Key provisions would include: authorizing HHS to distribute grant funding to eligible entities for training programs, establishing the framework for how these grants would be administered, and supporting the development of community-based mental health workforce capacity.
