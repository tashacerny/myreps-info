summary: "# Bill Summary: Export Control Reform Act Penalty Amendment  This bill proposes to increase the civil penalties that the federal government can impose on individuals and entities that violate export control regulations established under the Export Control Reform Act of 2018. Export controls restrict the sale or transfer of certain goods, technology, and services to foreign countries or entities, typically for national security, foreign policy, or nonproliferation reasons. By increasing penalty amounts, the bill aims to strengthen enforcement of these regulations.  The bill would primarily affect companies, individuals, and organizations involved in international trade and the export of controlled items. This includes manufacturers, distributors, technology firms, and other businesses subject to export licensing requirements. Violators of export control laws could face higher financial penalties under this proposal.  While the specific penalty amounts are not detailed in the available information, such amendments typically adjust penalty figures to account for inflation or to make violations costlier and thus more deterrent. The changes would be administered by relevant agencies such as the Department of Commerce, State Department, or Treasury Department, depending on the type of controlled item involved."
