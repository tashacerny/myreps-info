summary: "# Bill Summary: Medicare Payment for Radiologist Assistant Services  This bill proposes to amend the Medicare program (Title XVIII of the Social Security Act) to establish payment coverage for services provided by radiologist assistants. Currently, Medicare does not have a defined payment mechanism for these services. The bill would create a new reimbursement pathway, allowing radiologist assistants to bill Medicare directly for their work in diagnostic imaging and radiological procedures.  **Who It Affects:** - Radiologist assistants (healthcare professionals who work under radiologist supervision to perform imaging procedures and assist with patient care) - Medicare beneficiaries seeking radiological services - Healthcare facilities and imaging centers that employ radiologist assistants - Radiologists who supervise these professionals  **Key Provisions:** The bill establishes Medicare payment for radiologist assistant services, though the specific payment rates, eligible services, and supervision requirements would be determined through the rulemaking process. This change would align Medicare policy with current practice in many healthcare settings, where radiologist assistants play an active role in diagnostic imaging. The bill may also include provisions addressing how these services are coded, billed, and the conditions under which they qualify for reimbursement."
