summary: "# Bill Summary: National Breast Cancer Awareness Month (October 2025)  This bill proposes to officially recognize October 2025 as \"National Breast Cancer Awareness Month\" at the federal level. The measure is a commemorative designation that would acknowledge the month traditionally associated with breast cancer awareness efforts and education in the United States.  The bill would affect federal recognition and messaging, allowing government agencies and officials to formally support and promote awareness activities during the designated month. This type of designation typically does not create new funding, programs, or regulatory requirements, but rather serves as an official acknowledgment that can be used to highlight the importance of breast cancer awareness, prevention, screening, and support for those affected by the disease.  Key provisions would likely include the formal designation of the month and potentially encourage federal agencies, public institutions, and organizations to participate in awareness activities. Such recognitions are commonly used to draw public attention to health issues and support existing nonprofit and medical community efforts focused on education, research, and patient support."
