summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill would amend federal transportation law to limit the legal liability of motor vehicle manufacturers. Specifically, it would prohibit manufacturers from being held responsible under common law (judge-made law based on court precedents) for failing to manufacture or equip vehicles beyond what is required by applicable federal motor vehicle safety standards. In other words, if a vehicle meets all federal safety requirements, manufacturers could not be sued for injuries or damages based on claims that the vehicle should have included additional safety features beyond those mandates.  The bill would primarily affect motor vehicle manufacturers and consumers. Manufacturers would gain protection from lawsuits claiming their vehicles lack safety features that exceed federal requirements. Consumers and accident victims could be limited in their ability to sue manufacturers for injuries if the vehicles met existing federal standards, even if a court might have previously found the manufacturer negligent for not providing extra safety measures.  The scope and detailed provisions of this bill cannot be fully assessed without the complete legislative text, but the core mechanism would establish that federal safety standards serve as a ceiling for manufacturer liability rather than a floor, potentially narrowing the grounds on which injured parties can bring safety-based lawsuits against vehicle manufacturers in state courts."
