summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill would require candidates for federal office to divest from publicly traded securities or place them in a qualified blind trust once they file their candidacy. The requirement applies to candidates seeking positions in the U.S. House of Representatives, Senate, and presidency. The bill aims to address potential conflicts of interest by preventing candidates and elected officials from holding direct financial interests in publicly traded companies while in office or campaigning for office.  The bill would affect anyone filing to run for federal elected office. It establishes two compliance options: candidates could either sell their publicly traded securities before taking office, or transfer them to a blind trust arrangement where an independent trustee manages the investments without the officeholder's knowledge of specific holdings. This structure is intended to prevent candidates from making decisions—whether legislative, regulatory, or executive—that could benefit their personal financial interests.  The bill's stated purpose, indicated by its subtitle \"and for other purposes,\" suggests it may contain additional provisions beyond the core divestment requirement, though those specifics cannot be detailed without the full text. The measure would represent a significant ethics standard for federal candidates, similar to existing conflict-of-interest rules that apply to federal employees in certain positions."
