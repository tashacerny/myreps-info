summary: "# Summary: Warehouse Worker Recognition Week Resolution  This resolution expresses congressional support for designating August 17–23, 2026, as \"Warehouse Worker Recognition Week.\" The measure aims to recognize and celebrate workers employed in the logistics and warehouse industry during that specific week.  The resolution primarily affects workers in the warehouse and logistics sectors, including those involved in storage, distribution, inventory management, and related supply chain operations. While the resolution itself does not create new programs or funding, it serves as a formal acknowledgment of the contributions these workers make to the economy.  The key provision is the designation of the specified week in August 2026 as an official recognition period. Resolutions of this type are typically ceremonial in nature and do not carry the force of law, but they allow Congress to officially acknowledge particular groups, professions, or causes."
