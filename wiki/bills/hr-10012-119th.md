summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill proposes to amend Medicare regulations to require that the \"Medicare & You\" handbook include information about how enrollment in the Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) patient enrollment system affects Medicare premiums. The handbook is an annual publication distributed to Medicare beneficiaries with information about their coverage and benefits. The bill seeks to ensure that Medicare beneficiaries who are also eligible for VA benefits understand the financial implications of enrolling in the VA system, particularly regarding how such enrollment may impact their Medicare premium payments.  The bill primarily affects Medicare beneficiaries who are veterans or otherwise eligible for VA health services. It also affects the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS), which produces the Medicare & You handbook annually. By adding this information to the handbook, the bill aims to improve transparency and help beneficiaries make informed decisions about using both Medicare and VA health benefits simultaneously.  The key provision requires the Medicare & You handbook to disclose information about the relationship between VA patient enrollment and Medicare premiums. This would help eligible veterans understand potential cost differences and interactions between the two benefit systems before making enrollment decisions."
