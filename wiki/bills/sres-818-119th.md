summary: "# Bill Summary  This resolution designates July as \"National Anti-Counterfeiting and Consumer Education and Awareness Month.\" The measure is symbolic in nature and does not establish new laws or regulations. Instead, it expresses congressional recognition of trademarks' economic importance and their role in protecting consumers from counterfeit goods.  The resolution affects trademark holders, consumers, and businesses that rely on trademark protections. By establishing this observance month, it aims to raise public awareness about counterfeit products and the risks they may pose to consumer safety. The designation encourages educational efforts and dialogue about trademark protection and counterfeiting during July.  Key aspects include acknowledgment of trademarks as valuable intellectual property that help businesses build consumer trust and brand identity, and recognition that counterfeit goods can pose safety risks to consumers. The resolution serves primarily as an awareness and advocacy tool rather than a substantive policy change."
