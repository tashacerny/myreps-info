summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill would direct the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to establish and maintain a minimum number of Vet Centers in each state. Vet Centers are Department of Veterans Affairs facilities that provide mental health counseling, readjustment services, and support to veterans and their families. The bill aims to ensure more consistent geographic access to these mental health services across the country by setting a floor for the number of centers required in each state.  The bill would affect veterans seeking mental health care and readjustment services, as well as the Veterans Affairs Department's resource allocation and operations. It would require the VA to evaluate current Vet Center distribution and potentially open or relocate facilities to meet the minimum state requirements.  Key provisions would include establishing the minimum threshold for Vet Centers per state and directing the VA Secretary to implement this requirement. The specific minimum number per state is not detailed in the title, but the intent is to improve mental health care access for veterans nationwide by reducing geographic disparities in service availability."
