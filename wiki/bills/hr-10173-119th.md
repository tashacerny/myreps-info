The REU program provides funding for undergraduate students to participate in hands-on research projects across science, technology, engineering, and mathematics (STEM) fields at universities, national laboratories, and other research institutions. The bill amends the America COMPETES Reauthorization Act of 2010, which is the primary federal legislation governing NSF activities and funding levels.  The reauthorization would extend the program's authorization for a specified period, allowing it to continue operating and receiving federal appropriations. This affects undergraduate students seeking research opportunities, colleges and universities that host REU sites, and the NSF's ability to administer the program.
