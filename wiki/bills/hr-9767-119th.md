summary: "# Summary: Craft Distilled Spirits Direct-to-Consumer Shipping Act of 2026  **What the Bill Proposes** This bill would establish a framework allowing craft distilled spirits producers to ship products directly to consumers across state lines. Currently, federal law and many state laws restrict or prohibit direct-to-consumer shipment of spirits, requiring distribution through licensed wholesalers and retailers instead. This legislation would create new pathways for smaller distilleries to bypass traditional distribution channels and sell directly to customers.  **Who It Affects** The bill would primarily benefit craft distillery producers—likely defined by production capacity or ownership criteria—who want to expand their market reach without relying on wholesale networks. Consumers in states that allow such shipments would gain access to products from out-of-state producers. The bill would also affect licensed alcohol wholesalers and retailers, who currently serve as mandatory intermediaries in most states, as well as state alcohol regulatory agencies tasked with implementing new shipping rules.  **Key Provisions (Expected)** While the full text is unavailable, bills of this type typically include: eligibility criteria for participating distilleries (usually based on annual production volume); shipping restrictions to states where it is legally permitted; age verification and signature requirements; record-keeping and reporting obligations to tax authorities; and provisions addressing how direct shipments interact with existing state laws and tax collection systems."
