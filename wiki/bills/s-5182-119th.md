summary: "# Bill Summary: Federal Working Group for Recycled Plastic Content Traceability  This bill proposes establishing a federal working group tasked with developing standards and methods to track recycled plastic content in products. The working group would work to create systems that can verify and trace plastic materials as they move through manufacturing and supply chains, helping to confirm when products actually contain the recycled content they claim to have.  The bill would primarily affect plastic manufacturers, product producers, recyclers, and federal agencies involved in environmental or trade oversight. By creating traceability standards, the legislation aims to address concerns about \"greenwashing\"—when companies make unverified claims about recycled content—and to improve transparency in the plastics industry.  Key provisions would likely include establishing the working group's composition (such as representation from industry, government, and environmental sectors), defining its scope of work, setting timelines for developing traceability standards, and possibly requiring the group to submit recommendations to Congress. The bill may also address how these standards could be implemented and monitored across the industry."
