Hubble Post Office Designation  This bill proposes to officially rename the United States Postal Service facility located at 203 North Clay Street in Marshfield, Missouri. Under the bill, this post office would be designated as the \"Edwin P. Hubble Post Office.\" The measure is a naming designation and does not change the facility's operations, services, or functions.  The bill affects the postal facility itself and its official designation. It honors Edwin P. Hubble, for whom the facility would be named. Naming bills of this type are typically ceremonial in nature and serve to recognize individuals with historical or local significance. The renamed post office would continue to operate as a standard USPS facility serving the Marshfield, Missouri community."
