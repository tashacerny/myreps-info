summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill would require the Secretary of the Army to develop and issue guidance for reviewing applications related to hydropower projects. Specifically, the guidance would address how the Army evaluates requests for alterations to hydropower facilities, as well as applications for temporary or permanent occupation or use of these projects. The bill aims to establish clearer procedures and standards for this review process.  The legislation would primarily affect hydropower project operators, developers, and other entities seeking to modify or use Army Corps of Engineers-regulated hydropower facilities. It would also impact the Army Corps of Engineers, which would need to create and implement the new guidance framework.  Key provisions would likely include establishing uniform review criteria, timelines, and standards for processing these applications. By formalizing the guidance, the bill intends to create more transparency and consistency in how the Army Corps evaluates hydropower-related requests, potentially reducing uncertainty for applicants and improving administrative efficiency."
