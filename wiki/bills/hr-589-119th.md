summary: "# Bill Summary: FACE Act Repeal Act of 2025  **What the bill proposes:** This bill would repeal the Freedom of Access to Clinic Entrances (FACE) Act of 1994, a federal law that criminalizes certain forms of violence, threats, and obstruction targeting abortion clinics, reproductive health facilities, and healthcare providers. The repeal would eliminate federal criminal penalties and civil remedies established under that law.  **Who it affects:** The repeal would affect abortion providers and clinics, other reproductive health facilities, healthcare workers at these locations, and individuals who use these services. It would also impact law enforcement agencies that currently enforce FACE Act provisions. Additionally, it could affect advocacy groups on various sides of abortion-related issues, as the law currently restricts certain protest activities at clinic entrances.  **Key provisions:** By repealing the FACE Act, the bill would remove federal prohibitions on: (1) use of force, threats, or property destruction targeting abortion facilities or providers; (2) intentionally obstructing or intimidating individuals seeking or providing abortion services; and (3) damaging or destroying reproductive health clinics. The repeal would shift enforcement authority to state and local law enforcement under existing state laws, which vary significantly in their coverage and penalties for such conduct."
