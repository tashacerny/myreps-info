The bill directs the federal government to establish a strategy for how the U.S. will support the development, deployment, and advocacy of these technologies domestically and internationally.  The bill would affect multiple stakeholders, including federal agencies responsible for communications policy, private sector companies that develop and deploy wireless technologies, and consumers who use Wi-Fi and unlicensed spectrum services. It may also influence international technology standards and competitiveness discussions.  Key provisions would likely include requirements for the Commerce Department to assess the current state of unlicensed technologies, identify barriers to their advancement, and outline specific actions the federal government will take to support their growth. The plan would presumably address policy recommendations, coordination among federal agencies, and strategies for maintaining U.S. leadership in wireless technology development and standards-setting."
