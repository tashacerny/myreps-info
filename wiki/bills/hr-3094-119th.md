summary: "# PREP Act Summary  I can provide a more accurate summary with additional context. The \"PREP Act\" most commonly refers to the **Public Readiness and Emergency Preparedness Act of 2005**. This federal law allows the Secretary of Health and Human Services to issue declarations that provide liability protections and other measures during public health emergencies.  **What it proposes:** Under a PREP Act declaration, the Secretary of HHS can temporarily authorize unapproved medical treatments, vaccines, or devices; expedite their distribution; and provide liability protections to manufacturers, distributors, healthcare providers, and others involved in administering covered countermeasures. The act aims to encourage rapid response to emerging health threats by reducing legal barriers.  **Who it affects:** The law applies to manufacturers and suppliers of medical products, healthcare workers and facilities administering treatments, state and local health officials, and the general public receiving emergency medical countermeasures during declared public health emergencies.  **Key provisions:** Declarations grant immunity from liability (except in cases of willful misconduct) to covered entities; allow the use of products that haven't completed normal FDA approval; and permit flexibility in how and where treatments are distributed. Individuals harmed may seek compensation through the federal Countermeasures Injury Compensation Program rather than through civil lawsuits.
