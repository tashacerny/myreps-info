summary: "# Summary of the Ending Restaurant Purchases with SNAP Act of 2026  **What the Bill Proposes**  Based on its title, this bill would eliminate the ability to use SNAP benefits (food stamps) to purchase prepared food from restaurants. Currently, SNAP recipients in certain states can use their benefits at authorized restaurants through a Restaurant Meals Program. This bill would end that program nationwide, restricting SNAP benefits to purchases of unprepared groceries only.  **Who It Affects**  The bill would directly affect SNAP recipients who currently use benefits at participating restaurants, as well as restaurants authorized to accept SNAP benefits. It would also impact elderly, disabled, and homeless individuals who may rely on the Restaurant Meals Program when they lack cooking facilities or access to grocery stores. State governments administering SNAP would need to adjust their programs accordingly.  **Key Provisions**  While the full text is unavailable, the bill's primary provision would be prohibiting SNAP redemption at restaurants. This would effectively eliminate the existing Restaurant Meals Program, which operates in a limited number of states and is currently available only to elderly, disabled, and homeless SNAP recipients. The bill may include transition provisions or implementation timelines, though these cannot be confirmed without the full text."
