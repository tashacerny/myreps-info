summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill recognizes the 100-year history of American Legion Baseball and the American Legion World Series, which is a national youth baseball tournament. The bill also designates Shelby, North Carolina, as the permanent home of the American Legion World Series. American Legion Baseball is a youth sports program sponsored by the American Legion, a veterans' organization, and has operated as a competitive baseball league for young players across the United States.  The bill primarily affects Shelby, North Carolina, by formally recognizing it as the host city for the American Legion World Series tournament. The bill honors the city's role in hosting this annual event, which brings together youth baseball teams from across the country. The legislation also recognizes the American Legion's century-long contribution to youth baseball development and competition in America.  As a recognitional measure, this bill does not establish new funding, regulations, or substantive policy changes. Instead, it serves to commemorate a milestone in American Legion Baseball's history and formally acknowledge Shelby's status as the tournament's permanent home."
