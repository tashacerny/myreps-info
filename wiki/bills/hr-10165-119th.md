summary: "# Bill Summary: Export-Import Bank Quorum Requirements Reform  This bill proposes to permanently modify the quorum requirements for the Board of Directors of the Export-Import Bank of the United States (Ex-Im Bank). A quorum is the minimum number of board members who must be present to conduct official business. By lowering or adjusting these requirements, the bill aims to ensure the bank can continue operating and making decisions even when board seats are vacant or members are unavailable.  The bill would affect the Ex-Im Bank, a federal agency that provides financing and insurance to support U.S. exports, as well as exporters and businesses that rely on the bank's services. The primary beneficiaries would be companies seeking export financing and the bank's operational capacity. The key provision is the permanent change to quorum rules, which would reduce barriers to board action and maintain the bank's ability to function during periods when it might otherwise lack sufficient board members to meet current requirements."
