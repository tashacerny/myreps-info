summary: "# Summary: American Grown Flower and Foliage Month Resolution  This resolution designates July 2026 as \"American Grown Flower and Foliage Month\" at the federal level. The measure is ceremonial in nature and does not create new laws, regulations, or funding programs. Instead, it expresses congressional recognition of the domestic flower and foliage industry and encourages awareness of domestically grown floral and plant products.  The designation would primarily affect the flower and foliage industry, including growers, wholesalers, and retailers of American-grown flowers and plants. The resolution serves to highlight and promote this agricultural sector during the designated month. It may support marketing efforts and consumer awareness about domestic floral products, though the resolution itself carries no enforcement mechanism or budgetary impact."
