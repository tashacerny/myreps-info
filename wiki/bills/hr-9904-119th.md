summary: "# Bill Summary: Catalytic Converter Trafficking Prohibition  This bill proposes to amend federal law (title 18, United States Code) to create criminal prohibitions related to the trafficking of catalytic converters. Catalytic converters are emissions control devices installed on vehicles that contain precious metals, including platinum, palladium, and rhodium. The bill aims to address the theft and illegal resale of these devices, which has become a widespread crime problem in recent years.  The bill would likely affect vehicle owners, auto repair shops, scrap metal dealers, and law enforcement agencies. Vehicle owners are impacted by catalytic converter theft, which damages their vehicles and creates safety and repair costs. Scrap metal dealers and recycling businesses would face new regulations regarding the purchase and sale of catalytic converters. The legislation would also provide law enforcement with new tools to prosecute trafficking offenses.  Key provisions would typically include criminal penalties for trafficking in catalytic converters, requirements for proper documentation and identification when buying or selling converters, and possibly restrictions on who can legally purchase or sell them. The bill may also establish record-keeping requirements for dealers to create a traceable chain of custody, helping distinguish legally obtained converters from stolen ones."
