summary: "# Summary: No PFAS in Cosmetics Act  **What the Bill Proposes** This bill would restrict or ban the use of PFAS (per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances) in cosmetic products. PFAS are a large group of human-made chemicals that are used in various consumer products for their water- and stain-resistant properties. The legislation aims to limit consumers' exposure to these chemicals through cosmetics by preventing their inclusion in products like makeup, skincare items, and similar consumer goods.  **Who It Affects** The bill would affect cosmetics manufacturers and distributors, who would need to reformulate products to remove PFAS ingredients, and consumers who use cosmetic products. Regulatory agencies like the FDA would be responsible for implementing and enforcing the restrictions.  **Key Provisions** While the full text is unavailable, such legislation typically includes: a prohibition on PFAS in cosmetic products (either complete or above certain concentration thresholds); compliance timelines for manufacturers to reformulate existing products; labeling or disclosure requirements; and enforcement mechanisms with penalties for violations. The bill may also include exemptions for specific uses or allow for phase-in periods to give manufacturers time to adjust their production processes."
