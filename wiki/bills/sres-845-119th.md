summary: "# Summary of National Child Awareness Month Resolution  This resolution designates September 2026 as \"National Child Awareness Month\" at the federal level. The designation is intended to increase public awareness of charities and youth-serving organizations operating throughout the United States. The resolution recognizes these organizations' work on behalf of children and youth as significant contributions to the nation's future.  The resolution affects charitable organizations, youth-serving groups, and potentially the general public by providing an official month for promoting awareness of these entities and their missions. During this designated month, these organizations could use the federal recognition to highlight their work and encourage public engagement and support.  The resolution does not establish funding, create new programs, or impose requirements on organizations. It is a symbolic measure that designates a specific time period for recognizing and promoting awareness of existing charities and youth-serving organizations already operating in the United States."
