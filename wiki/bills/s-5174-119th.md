summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill proposes to amend the Richard B. Russell National School Lunch Act by requiring school food authorities to publicly disclose any waivers they receive from the \"Buy American\" requirement. The Buy American provision generally requires that school lunch programs prioritize purchasing food products that are produced or manufactured in the United States. When schools request and receive waivers from this requirement, the bill would mandate that these waivers be made available to the public.  The bill affects school food authorities—the agencies responsible for administering school meal programs—as well as parents, taxpayers, and community members who would gain access to information about waiver decisions. The key provision requires transparency by making waiver information public, which would allow stakeholders to see when and why schools have chosen to source food from non-domestic suppliers instead of complying with the Buy American standard.  The stated purpose, based on the bill's language, is to increase transparency and accountability in school meal procurement decisions. This would give the public visibility into instances where schools have determined that domestic products are unavailable, cost-prohibitive, or otherwise unable to meet their needs, or when other circumstances warrant an exception to the Buy American requirement."
