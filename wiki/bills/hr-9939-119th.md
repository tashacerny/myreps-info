summary: "# Bill Summary: Prohibition on AI Data Centers on Federal Land  **What the Bill Proposes**  This bill would prohibit the construction and operation of artificial intelligence (AI) data centers on federal land. The legislation would prevent federal property from being used or leased for facilities designed primarily to support AI computing operations, including the infrastructure needed to train and run large-scale AI models.  **Who It Affects**  The bill would impact technology companies and AI developers that might otherwise use federal land for data center development. It would also affect federal land management agencies (such as the Department of Interior and Forest Service) that currently have authority to lease or permit activities on public lands. Additionally, it could influence broader AI infrastructure development strategy in the United States by restricting where such facilities can be sited.  **Key Provisions**  The bill creates a categorical prohibition, meaning AI data centers would be excluded from permitted uses on federal property without exception. The legislation does not appear to address existing facilities, grandfather clauses, or define specific technical standards for what qualifies as an \"AI data center,\" though these details would likely be clarified in the full text. The bill's scope would apply across all federal lands managed by various agencies."
