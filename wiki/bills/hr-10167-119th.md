summary: "# Bill Summary: Penny Discontinuation and Cash Rounding  This bill would direct the Secretary of the Treasury to cease minting penny coins and establish a rounding system for cash transactions. Under the proposed rounding mechanism, cash purchases would be rounded to the nearest 5 cents—rounded up or down depending on the final digit of the transaction amount. For example, a purchase totaling $5.01–$5.02 would round down to $5.00, while $5.03–$5.07 would round to $5.05. This rounding would apply only to cash transactions; electronic payments would not be affected.  The bill primarily affects consumers, retailers, and the Treasury Department. Consumers who use cash would experience rounding on their transactions, potentially resulting in minor savings or costs depending on purchase amounts. Retailers would need to adjust their point-of-sale procedures to implement the rounding system. The Treasury Department would eliminate penny production costs, though the long-term fiscal impact would depend on the volume of pennies currently in circulation and production expenses.  The proposal addresses longstanding debate about penny production, which costs the Treasury more than the penny's face value to manufacture. However, the bill's implementation would require coordination between multiple agencies and retailers to ensure consistent rounding practices across the country."
