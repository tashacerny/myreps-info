summary: "# Summary: Sunset for the 2001 Authorization for Use of Military Force Act  This bill would establish an expiration date—a \"sunset provision\"—for the 2001 Authorization for Use of Military Force (AUMF). The 2001 AUMF, passed shortly after the September 11 terrorist attacks, granted the President broad authority to use military force against al-Qaeda, the Taliban, and associated forces. The bill would require Congress to reauthorize this military authority after a specified period, rather than allowing it to remain in effect indefinitely.  The legislation affects the executive branch's military powers and any armed forces currently operating under the 2001 AUMF authority. It would also impact Congress, which would need to actively vote on renewal if it wants military operations to continue past the sunset date. The key provision is the establishment of an automatic expiration date, after which the President would lose the legal authority granted by the original 2001 resolution unless Congress passes new legislation to continue or replace it.  This type of provision reflects ongoing legislative debate about congressional oversight of military actions and the appropriate duration of war authorizations. Without seeing the full text, the specific sunset date and any transition provisions cannot be detailed, but the core mechanism would shift control over military authority back toward requiring explicit congressional action rather than relying on a standing authorization from over two decades earlier."
