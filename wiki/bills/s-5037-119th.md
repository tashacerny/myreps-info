summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill would amend the Medicaid program (Title XIX of the Social Security Act) to expand eligibility for children and young adults. Specifically, it would make all children eligible for Medicaid coverage from birth through age 25, regardless of family income or other current eligibility requirements. This represents a significant expansion from current law, which ties Medicaid eligibility to income thresholds and other state-specific factors.  The bill would also require states to automatically enroll eligible children and young adults under age 26 in their state Medicaid programs, rather than requiring families to apply for coverage. This automatic enrollment provision is intended to reduce administrative barriers and increase the number of eligible individuals who actually receive coverage.  The bill would affect states (which administer Medicaid) and all children and young adults under age 26 in the United States. It would also have budgetary implications for both federal and state governments, as expanded Medicaid coverage requires increased funding. The phrase \"and for other purposes\" suggests the bill may contain additional provisions not evident from the title alone."
