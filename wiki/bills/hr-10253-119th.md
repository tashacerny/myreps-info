Lawrence Brown Post Office Building Designation  This bill proposes to designate a specific U.S. Postal Service facility located at 1706 South Burnside Avenue in Gonzales, Louisiana, with an official name: the \"Cpl. Lawrence Brown Post Office Building.\" The designation would honor Corporal Lawrence Brown through the naming of this postal facility.  The bill affects the Postal Service facility in Gonzales and establishes a formal commemorative name for the building. The designation is primarily symbolic and does not change the facility's operations or functions—it serves to recognize and honor the individual for whom the building is named. Such designations typically require Congressional approval and are common ways to honor military service members or other individuals of significance."
