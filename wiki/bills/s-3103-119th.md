summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill would authorize the extension of nondiscriminatory trade treatment—formally known as Normal Trade Relations (NTR) status—to certain countries. NTR status is a fundamental principle of international trade law that requires countries to treat imports from one trading partner the same way they treat imports from any other trading partner. By authorizing NTR extension, this bill would allow specified countries to access the same tariff rates and trade terms as most other nations in U.S. commerce.  The bill primarily affects U.S. trade policy and the designated countries' access to the American market. It would benefit exporters and businesses in the recipient countries by providing them with competitive tariff treatment, and could expand trade opportunities for U.S. importers and consumers. The legislation may also have effects on domestic industries that compete with imports from these countries.
