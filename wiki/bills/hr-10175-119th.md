The bill would also nullify (invalidate) any tariffs or duties that have been imposed by Presidential proclamation under this specific legal authority. The bill is framed as a legislative action to remove this Presidential power going forward and to undo existing tariffs that were put in place using it.  The bill would affect importers, exporters, businesses reliant on imported goods, and consumers who may be impacted by tariff changes. It would also limit executive branch authority over trade policy in this particular area, shifting decision-making power back to Congress, which has primary constitutional authority over tariffs and trade regulation.
