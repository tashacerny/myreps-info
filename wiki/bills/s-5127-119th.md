summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill would modify the Internal Revenue Code to exclude certain compensation paid to clinical trial participants from their taxable gross income. Currently, payment for participating in clinical trials is generally treated as taxable income. Under this proposal, participants could receive compensation for their trial participation without reporting it as income for federal tax purposes, up to certain limits or conditions specified in the legislation.  The bill would primarily affect individuals who participate in clinical trials and the research organizations or sponsors who conduct them. By reducing the tax burden on trial participants, the bill aims to make clinical trial participation more financially attractive to potential volunteers. This could increase recruitment and participation in medical research studies, which rely on volunteer subjects to test new drugs, medical devices, and treatment approaches.  The exact scope and limitations of the exclusion would depend on the specific provisions included in the full bill text, which may address matters such as: dollar limits on excluded compensation, types of trials covered, documentation requirements, and any other conditions or definitions necessary to implement the exclusion."
