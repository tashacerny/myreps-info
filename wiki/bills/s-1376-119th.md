summary: "# Summary of Benton MacKaye National Scenic Trail Feasibility Study Act of 2025  This bill would authorize a feasibility study for a new National Scenic Trail to be named after Benton MacKaye, a regional planner and conservationist. The study would evaluate the practicality, costs, and benefits of establishing this trail as an official component of the National Scenic Trails System managed by the National Park Service or U.S. Forest Service. The bill would direct relevant federal agencies to assess the proposed route, identify necessary land agreements, and evaluate environmental and recreational impacts.  The study would affect federal agencies involved in trail management and land administration, as well as private landowners and local communities along the proposed route. State and local governments may also be involved in planning discussions. The bill would provide a mechanism for determining whether the trail is feasible and desirable before committing to its full development and maintenance.  Key provisions would likely include authorization for the study itself, a timeline for completion, funding for the feasibility assessment, and requirements for agency coordination and public input. Once completed, the study would inform future decisions about whether to formally designate the Benton MacKaye National Scenic Trail."
