summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill proposes to designate a specific United States Postal Service facility as the \"Master Sergeant Melvin O. Handrich Post Office Building.\" The facility is located at 306 South Main Street in Waupaca, Wisconsin. The designation is a formal naming that honors Master Sergeant Melvin O. Handrich through the post office building in his community.  The bill affects the Waupaca post office and residents of Waupaca, Wisconsin, who use this facility. The naming designation itself does not change the post office's operations, services, or hours—it is a commemorative action that officially associates the building with Master Sergeant Handrich's name and legacy.  This type of legislation is a common practice in Congress. It typically honors individuals with military service, significant community contributions, or other forms of public service. The practical effect is limited to official signage and documentation; the post office will continue to serve its normal postal functions under the new official name."
