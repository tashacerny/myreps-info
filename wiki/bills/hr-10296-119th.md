The legislation is a designating measure, meaning it assigns a specific name to an existing federal building rather than creating new programs or policies.  The designation would affect the postal facility and its operations in Fort Worth. The bill would apply primarily to the building itself and how it is officially referred to in federal records and documentation. No operational changes to postal services or budget impacts would result from this naming designation.  This type of legislation honors an individual by memorializing their name on a federal building. Such designations are common in Congress and typically require a simple majority vote to pass."
