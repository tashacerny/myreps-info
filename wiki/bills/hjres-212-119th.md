summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill uses the Congressional Review Act (CRA) to disapprove a rule issued by the Environmental Protection Agency (EPA). The EPA rule addresses California's motor vehicle pollution control standards under its \"Advanced Clean Car Program.\" Specifically, the rule reconsiders the EPA's previous withdrawal of a waiver that had allowed California to set its own vehicle emissions standards rather than follow federal standards. The bill would invalidate the EPA's decision through a congressional disapproval process.  The rule affects automakers, California, and potentially other states that follow California's emissions standards. Under the Clean Air Act, California can request a waiver to establish stricter vehicle pollution standards than federal requirements; other states can then choose to follow either federal or California standards. The EPA rule being challenged would restore or modify the conditions under which California can enforce its own standards through the Advanced Clean Car Program, which typically includes requirements related to vehicle emissions and zero-emission vehicle adoption.  If passed, this bill would prohibit the EPA from issuing the rule in substantially the same form unless Congress specifically authorizes it. The Congressional Review Act allows Congress to overturn recent federal regulations through this expedited process, though such measures require presidential approval to take effect."
