summary: "# Bill Summary: 988 Day Recognition  This bill is a recognition measure that designates September 8, 2026, as \"988 Day\" and acknowledges suicide as a serious public health issue. The bill expresses congressional support for the 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, a national telephone and text service that provides crisis support and suicide prevention resources to people in distress.  The measure affects the general public by raising awareness about suicide prevention and mental health crisis services. It does not create new programs or allocate funding, but rather uses the designated day to draw attention to existing resources. The 988 Lifeline, which the bill supports, is available to anyone experiencing suicidal thoughts, mental health crises, or emotional distress and is staffed by trained counselors.  Key provisions include the formal recognition of the date and the expression of support for the 988 Lifeline's role in addressing mental health crises. By designating a specific day for this purpose, the bill aims to promote public awareness of suicide prevention efforts and encourage people in crisis to utilize available mental health services."
