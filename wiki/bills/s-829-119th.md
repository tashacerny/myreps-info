[Bill Number]  This bill proposes to designate a specific United States Postal Service facility located at 840 Front Street in Casselton, North Dakota, as the \"Commander Delbert Austin Olson Post Office.\" The legislation is a naming designation bill, which is a common type of legislation that honors individuals by naming federal buildings or facilities after them.  The bill would affect the postal facility in Casselton, North Dakota, which would be officially renamed to honor Commander Delbert Austin Olson. This type of designation is largely ceremonial and does not change the operational functions or services of the facility. The change would be reflected in official postal records and signage.  The key provision is simply the designation itself—renaming the postal facility at the specified address. No other operational, budgetary, or functional changes to the Postal Service are implied by the bill's title."
