summary: "# Summary of Congressional Disapproval Resolution  This resolution uses the Congressional Review Act (CRA) to disapprove an Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) rule granting California a waiver under the Clean Air Act. The waiver permits California to implement its own vehicle pollution control standards that are stricter than federal standards, specifically allowing California's Advanced Clean Car Program and Zero Emission Vehicle (ZEV) amendments for vehicles from 2017 and earlier model years. If passed, this resolution would invalidate the EPA's decision to grant the waiver, preventing California from enforcing these stricter state standards.  The resolution affects California's ability to regulate vehicle emissions within its borders, and indirectly impacts automotive manufacturers that would need to comply with California's standards if the waiver remains in effect. It also has implications for other states that often adopt California's vehicle pollution standards. The key provision is the disapproval of the EPA's waiver decision, which would revert California to compliance with federal emission standards rather than its own."
