summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill would designate Afghanistan as a country eligible for Temporary Protected Status (TPS) under section 244 of the Immigration and Nationality Act. TPS is a federal program that allows nationals of designated countries facing humanitarian crises—such as armed conflict, natural disasters, or epidemic disease—to live and work temporarily in the United States while conditions in their home country remain unsafe.  **Who It Affects**  The bill would affect Afghan nationals currently in the United States who are not lawful permanent residents or citizens. If designated for TPS, eligible Afghan nationals could apply for temporary protected status, which would allow them to remain in the country legally and obtain work authorization during the designation period. The designation could also prevent the deportation of eligible Afghan nationals to Afghanistan.  **Key Provisions**  By designating Afghanistan under section 244, the bill would enable the Secretary of Homeland Security to register Afghan nationals for TPS and provide them with documentation and work permits. TPS designations are temporary and typically last 6 to 18 months, though they can be extended or terminated depending on country conditions. The specific duration and conditions of the Afghan designation would be determined under existing TPS procedures, and the bill may include additional provisions related to security vetting or other administrative matters, though these are not detailed in the title alone."
