summary: "# Bill Summary: Public Financing Vouchers for Federal Elections  This bill would amend the Federal Election Campaign Act to establish a public financing system for federal elections based on voter-directed vouchers. Under this system, eligible voters would receive vouchers (likely in a specified dollar amount) that they could direct to federal candidates of their choice. This approach differs from traditional public financing systems, as it gives individual voters direct control over how public funds are allocated to candidates, rather than having funds distributed through a centralized mechanism.  The bill would affect candidates running for federal office (House, Senate, and potentially the presidency) who choose to participate in the voucher system, as well as all eligible voters who would receive and direct the vouchers. The system would also impact campaign finance more broadly by potentially changing how candidates raise and spend money in federal elections.  Key provisions would likely include eligibility requirements for both voters and participating candidates, the value of vouchers issued to each voter, procedures for voters to direct their vouchers, and how candidates would claim and use the funds. The bill may also address related matters such as matching funds, spending limits for participating candidates, and transition rules for implementation."
