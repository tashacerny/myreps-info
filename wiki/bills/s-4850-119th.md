summary: "# Summary: Diversity Jurisdiction Inflation Adjustment Act  **What the Bill Proposes**  This bill would adjust the monetary threshold for federal diversity jurisdiction in civil lawsuits. Diversity jurisdiction allows federal courts to hear cases between citizens of different states when the amount in controversy exceeds a specified dollar amount. The bill would increase this threshold to account for inflation, raising it from its current level (currently $75,000 since 1996) to a higher amount based on inflation adjustments.  **Who It Affects**  The change would affect private parties involved in interstate civil disputes, federal district courts handling caseloads, and potentially state courts that would see increased filings if fewer cases qualify for federal court. Businesses and individuals suing across state lines would be most directly impacted, as would the court systems involved.  **Key Implications**  By raising the diversity jurisdiction threshold, the bill would reduce the number of cases eligible for federal court review, potentially shifting more interstate disputes to state courts. Supporters of such measures typically argue this reduces federal court congestion and allows courts to focus on federal questions. The specific adjusted dollar amount would depend on the inflation calculation method chosen in the final bill text."
