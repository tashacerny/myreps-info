summary: "# Summary of Glioblastoma Awareness Day Resolution  This resolution expresses congressional support for designating July 16, 2025, as \"Glioblastoma Awareness Day.\" The measure is a symbolic statement rather than a law that creates new requirements or programs. It aims to increase public awareness about glioblastoma, a type of brain cancer.  The resolution affects patients with glioblastoma, their families and caregivers, medical professionals, and organizations focused on brain cancer research and advocacy. The designation would encourage public and private entities to recognize the date and promote education about the disease.  The key provision is the symbolic designation of the specific date as a national awareness day. Such resolutions typically do not have budgetary impacts or enforce new policies, but instead serve to highlight health issues and encourage voluntary awareness-raising activities by governments, nonprofits, healthcare providers, and the public."
