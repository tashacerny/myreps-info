summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill is a congressional resolution that officially recognizes suicide as a serious public health problem in the United States. The resolution expresses support for designating September 2026 as \"National Suicide Prevention Month\" and September 10, 2026, as \"World Suicide Prevention Day.\" These designations are symbolic recognitions intended to raise awareness and focus national attention on suicide prevention efforts.  The bill affects the general public, mental health organizations, healthcare providers, and advocacy groups working on suicide prevention. By establishing these official designations, the resolution aims to encourage public awareness campaigns, educational initiatives, and community support activities related to suicide prevention during that period.  The key provision is the formal congressional recognition and support for these observances. Such resolutions typically do not create new laws or allocate funding, but serve as statements of Congress acknowledging an issue and encouraging related awareness and action by government agencies, organizations, and the public."
