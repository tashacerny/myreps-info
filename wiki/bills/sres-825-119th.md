The resolution affects Mississippi Christian University by providing official state recognition of its historical significance and service. It may also affect state residents and alumni who have connections to the institution. However, as a commendatory measure, it does not impose requirements or changes on the university, state government, or other parties.  The key provision is the formal commendation itself—a statement acknowledging the university's 200-year history and its role serving the state and nation. Commendatory resolutions typically have no fiscal impact and do not establish binding obligations, but serve to document legislative recognition of an entity's achievements or milestones."
