summary: "# Bill Summary: Dual Enrollment in SNAP and Food Distribution Programs  This bill would amend the Food and Nutrition Act of 2008 to permit individuals to participate simultaneously in both the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) and the Food Distribution Program on Indian Reservations (FDPIR). Currently, these programs are generally structured as alternatives—households must choose one or the other. The proposed change would allow dual enrollment, meaning eligible households could receive benefits from both programs at the same time.  The bill would primarily affect Native American households living on Indian reservations that are eligible for federal nutrition assistance. It would also impact the administrative operations of both programs at the federal and tribal levels. The change could expand nutrition assistance available to low-income households in these communities, though it may also increase program costs and require administrative coordination between SNAP and FDPIR systems.  The specific mechanics of how dual enrollment would work—such as how benefits would be calculated, whether they would be combined or kept separate, and any limitations on participation—would be determined by the implementation details in the full bill language."
