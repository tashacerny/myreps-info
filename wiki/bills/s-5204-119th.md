The bill would carve out individual account plans from some of these restrictions.  The bill would primarily affect individuals who hold or manage individual account plans (such as IRAs or similar retirement savings vehicles) and the financial institutions or service providers that work with these plans. It could also affect the Internal Revenue Service's enforcement of these rules.  The key provision is the creation of a new exemption category that would allow certain transactions involving individual account plans that would otherwise be prohibited.
