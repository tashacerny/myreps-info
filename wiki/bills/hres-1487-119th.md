summary: "# Summary of Bill Recognizing the 1960 University of Missouri Tigers Football Team  This bill is a commemorative resolution that recognizes the 1960 University of Missouri Tigers football team for their accomplishments during that season. Specifically, the resolution acknowledges the team's undefeated regular season record, their victory in the Orange Bowl (a major college football bowl game), and their claim to the 1960 National Championship title.  The bill does not propose any new laws, regulations, or expenditures. Rather, it is a symbolic measure expressing Congressional recognition and honor for the historical athletic achievement of the University of Missouri football program. Such commemorative resolutions typically serve to formally acknowledge significant accomplishments and may be displayed or referenced by the institution being honored. The resolution affects the University of Missouri, its football program, and the individuals who were part of that 1960 team."
