The resolution does not create new law or establish programs; rather, it serves as a formal statement of recognition and commemoration by Congress. It affects no specific groups through direct policy changes but is intended to honor victims, recognize heroic actions, and underscore a national commitment to remembering the events and their consequences. The bill represents a ceremonial and symbolic action by the legislative body to mark this significant anniversary."
