Typical provisions in such legislation include: defining what constitutes an \"intimate visual depiction\"; establishing that sharing such images without consent is unlawful; creating remedies for victims (such as civil lawsuits for damages); and potentially authorizing law enforcement to investigate and prosecute violations. The bill may also address related conduct, such as threatening to distribute such images as a form of coercion or blackmail.  **Scope Considerations**  Depending on legislative details, the bill may specify exceptions (such as images shared for legitimate purposes, consent to limited distribution, or images that are matters of public concern), define the mental state required for liability, establish penalties, and clarify jurisdiction across state lines or digital platforms."
