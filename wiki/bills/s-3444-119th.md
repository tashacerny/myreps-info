summary: "# Summary of Bernie Bluestein Post Office Building Designation Bill  This bill proposes to designate the United States Postal Service facility located at 1300 East Northwest Highway in Palatine, Illinois, with an official name: the \"Bernie Bluestein Post Office Building.\" The designation is a ceremonial action that renames the existing postal facility in honor of Bernie Bluestein.  The bill affects the postal facility in Palatine and how it is officially referred to, though the designation does not change the facility's operations or functions. It impacts the USPS, the local Palatine community, and anyone who may reference or interact with this post office location. Post office building designations are typically used to honor individuals who have made significant contributions to their communities or public service."
