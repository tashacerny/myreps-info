summary: "# Summary of Color-Coding Intoxicating Drinks Bill  This bill directs two federal agencies—the Food and Drug Administration (under HHS) and the Alcohol and Tobacco Tax and Trade Bureau (under Treasury)—to develop and implement a color-coding system for certain alcoholic beverages. The stated purpose is to create a standardized visual labeling method to identify or categorize intoxicating drinks. The bill affects manufacturers, distributors, and retailers of alcoholic beverages, as well as consumers who purchase these products.  The key provision requires the two agencies to establish the color-coding system, though the bill title does not specify which beverages would be included, what the color categories would represent (such as alcohol content, type of beverage, or other characteristics), or the timeline for implementation. Presumably, the system would apply to beverages sold in the United States and would need to be adopted by industry participants. The bill includes a catch-all phrase (\"for other purposes\"), which suggests additional provisions beyond the color-coding requirement may be included in the full legislative text."
