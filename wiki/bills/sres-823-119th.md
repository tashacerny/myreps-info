summary: "# Summary of National Farmers Market Week Resolution  This resolution designates the week of August 2–8, 2026, as \"National Farmers Market Week.\" The measure is a commemorative resolution that does not create new law or funding but instead honors and raises awareness for farmers markets during a specific week.  The resolution affects farmers market operators, vendors, and customers, as well as federal, state, and local agencies that may participate in recognizing the week. It may encourage promotional activities, educational events, or media coverage highlighting the role of farmers markets in local communities. The designation applies only to the specified week in 2026 and does not impose requirements or restrictions on any person or entity.  Key provisions would include officially designating the week and potentially encouraging government agencies and private organizations to support activities that promote farmers markets and their benefits to consumers and local agriculture during that period."
