summary: "# Bill Summary: Gold Star Children's Day Resolution  This resolution proposes to designate August 1, 2026, as \"Gold Star Children's Day\" at the federal level. A Gold Star family is one that has lost a family member in military service to the United States. Gold Star children, specifically, are the children of service members who died while serving in the armed forces or from service-connected causes. This designation would officially recognize and honor these children.  The resolution affects Gold Star children and their families by providing federal recognition of their sacrifice and loss. The designation serves a symbolic purpose, raising awareness about the unique experiences and challenges faced by children who have lost a parent in military service. Such recognitions typically encourage public acknowledgment and may be used to organize commemorative events or activities on the designated day.  The resolution does not create new programs, funding, or legal obligations. Rather, it is a symbolic measure that establishes an official day of recognition within the federal calendar, similar to other designated national observance days."
