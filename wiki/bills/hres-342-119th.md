summary: "# Bill Summary: Diethylstilbestrol (DES) Recognition  **What the Bill Proposes** This bill seeks to formally recognize the historical use of diethylstilbestrol (DES) and acknowledge the health harms it caused to people who were exposed to it. DES was a synthetic estrogen medication prescribed between the 1930s and 1970s, primarily to pregnant women to prevent miscarriages, though it was later found to be ineffective for this purpose. The bill appears to be a recognition or commemorative measure rather than one establishing new programs or direct compensation.  **Who It Affects** The bill affects individuals exposed to DES, including women who took the drug while pregnant and their children (known as \"DES daughters\" and \"DES sons\"). DES exposure has been linked to various health conditions, including rare vaginal cancers, reproductive system abnormalities, infertility, and other complications. The recognition may also affect healthcare providers and researchers studying DES-related health outcomes.  **Key Provisions** While the full text is unavailable, bills of this type typically include official acknowledgment of the harm caused, documentation of the historical record, and sometimes recommendations for further research, healthcare screening, or support services.
