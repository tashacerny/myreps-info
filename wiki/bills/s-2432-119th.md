It may have minimal practical impact on postal services themselves, as naming designations are primarily ceremonial. However, it recognizes Paul Ignatius in an official capacity and may affect local community identity and signage at the location.  **Key Provision**  The sole substantive provision is the designation of the named postal facility. No changes to operations, funding, or postal services are indicated by the title.
