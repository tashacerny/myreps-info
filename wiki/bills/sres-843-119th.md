summary: "# Summary  This resolution expresses support for designating the first week of August 2026 as \"National Community Health Center Week\" in the United States. The resolution encourages all Americans to visit their local community health centers during this week and aims to recognize and celebrate the work of community health centers and their relationships with the communities they serve.  The resolution affects community health centers nationwide and their patients, as well as the general public. Community health centers are federally qualified health centers that provide primary care and preventive services to patients regardless of ability to pay. The resolution does not create new laws or programs but rather establishes a designated week for national recognition and public participation.  The key provision is the encouragement for Americans to visit their local community health centers during the designated week. By establishing this observance, the resolution seeks to raise public awareness about the services these centers provide and highlight the partnerships between health centers and their local communities."
