summary: "# Bill Summary  This joint resolution seeks to disapprove a rule issued by the Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) concerning California's vehicle emission standards and the Advanced Clean Car Program. Under the Congressional Review Act (chapter 8 of title 5, U.S. Code), Congress can vote to overturn federal agency rules within a set timeframe. This resolution specifically targets the EPA's decision to reinstate a waiver that allows California to set its own motor vehicle pollution control standards that differ from federal standards, rather than enforcing the previous withdrawal of that waiver.  The rule affects automobile manufacturers, California, other states that may adopt California's standards, and EPA enforcement of emissions regulations. The key provision is the resolution's disapproval mechanism, which—if passed by both chambers and signed by the President—would invalidate the EPA's decision to reconsider the waiver withdrawal. This would prevent California from maintaining its authority to establish independent vehicle emission standards under the Clean Air Act.  # Note  A complete summary would require the full bill text to identify additional provisions, effective dates, and any specific standards or timelines referenced."
