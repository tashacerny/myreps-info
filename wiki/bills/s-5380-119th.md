summary: "# Bill Summary: Export Control Violations Statute of Limitations  **What the Bill Proposes**  This bill would establish a ten-year statute of limitations for prosecuting violations of export control laws under the Export Control Reform Act of 2018. A statute of limitations sets a deadline for bringing legal charges; after that period expires, prosecution is barred. The bill would clarify the timeframe within which the federal government must initiate legal action against individuals or entities accused of unlawfully exporting controlled items.  **Who It Affects**  The bill would affect companies, individuals, and organizations engaged in the export of controlled goods and technologies. It would also impact federal prosecutors and enforcement agencies responsible for investigating and prosecuting export control violations. Additionally, it could affect defendants accused of such violations, as a defined statute of limitations determines how long they face potential prosecution risk.  **Key Provision**  The primary provision sets the statute of limitations period at ten years for export control violations. This establishes a specific legal deadline, replacing any ambiguity about how long enforcement agencies have to pursue such cases under the 2018 Export Control Reform Act."
