summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill would prohibit the distribution of federal education funding to schools and educational institutions that do not permit religious exemptions from vaccination requirements. Under current law, states and schools are permitted to set their own vaccination policies, though most allow medical and some allow religious or philosophical exemptions. This bill would create a federal funding condition requiring educational institutions to offer religious exemption options to comply with federal funding eligibility.  **Who It Affects:** The bill would directly impact public school districts, charter schools, private schools receiving federal funds, and higher education institutions that currently mandate vaccinations without religious exemptions. It would also affect states with vaccination requirements, as their schools would need to modify policies to maintain federal education funding eligibility.  **Key Provisions:** The bill establishes religious exemption access as a requirement for federal education funding. This would override or modify existing state and institutional vaccination policies that do not provide such exemptions. The scope of \"religious exemptions\" and enforcement mechanisms would depend on the bill's specific language, which is not available in this summary. Federal education funding programs potentially affected could include Title I funding, special education grants, and other education assistance programs distributed through federal agencies."
