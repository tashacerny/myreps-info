summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill proposes amendments to the Federal Power Act to establish clearer authority for the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (FERC) regarding the interconnection of large electrical loads to the nation's transmission system. Currently, the regulatory framework for connecting large power consumers (such as industrial facilities, data centers, or other major energy users) to the grid lacks explicit standards and procedures. The bill would clarify FERC's jurisdiction in this area and establish a defined process for how such interconnections are reviewed and approved.  The bill would primarily affect large electricity consumers seeking to connect directly to the high-voltage transmission system, as well as transmission operators and utilities managing these connections. It would also impact FERC, which would receive explicit regulatory authority and responsibility in this area. By establishing standardized procedures and technical requirements, the bill aims to create a more predictable and consistent process for large load interconnections across the country.  Key provisions would likely include defining what constitutes a \"large load,\" establishing technical and procedural standards for interconnection applications, setting timelines for FERC review, and clarifying cost allocation and responsibility for necessary system upgrades. These changes are intended to reduce uncertainty and streamline a process that has previously operated without explicit federal standards."
