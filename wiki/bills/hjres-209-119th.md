summary: "# Summary: Gold Star Father's Day Designation  This bill proposes to establish November 9 as an annual observance called \"Gold Star Father's Day\" in the United States. A Gold Star refers to families who have lost a military service member in combat or service-related circumstances. The designation would create a dedicated day to honor and recognize fathers who have lost children while serving in the military.  The bill affects military families, particularly fathers who have experienced the loss of a son or daughter in military service, as well as veterans' organizations and communities that commemorate military sacrifice. The designation would not create a federal holiday or mandate any specific observances, but rather establish an official day of recognition similar to other commemorative designations.  Key provisions would include officially designating November 9 annually for this purpose, which typically involves recognition through proclamations, ceremonies, and public awareness. This type of legislation generally encourages individuals, organizations, and government entities to acknowledge and honor the contribution and sacrifice of Gold Star families on the designated date."
