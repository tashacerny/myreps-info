summary: "# Bill Summary  **What the Bill Proposes**  This bill would amend federal law (Title 18, United States Code) to add rioting to the list of activities defined as \"racketeering activity\" under the Racketeer Influenced and Corrupt Organizations (RICO) Act. Currently, RICO defines specific predicate offenses—underlying criminal acts—that can be prosecuted as racketeering when committed as part of an organized enterprise. This change would allow rioting to be charged as a predicate offense under RICO.  **Who It Affects and Key Impact**  The bill would primarily affect prosecutors, defendants charged with rioting offenses, and potentially organizations or enterprises involved in coordinated rioting. By adding rioting to RICO predicate offenses, prosecutors would gain an additional legal tool to pursue charges against individuals or groups engaged in organized rioting. Individuals could face more serious charges and enhanced penalties if their rioting conduct is prosecuted under RICO, which carries potential sentences up to 20 years imprisonment. The bill may also expand the scope of who can be charged—RICO allows prosecution of individuals who organize, manage, or otherwise participate in criminal enterprises, even if they did not personally commit the underlying offense.  **Implementation Note**  The specific definition of \"rioting\" that would apply under this bill is not provided in the title alone. The actual bill text would clarify which existing legal definition of rioting applies or whether a new definition is created for this purpose."
