The scope includes production capabilities, testing procedures, and sustainment (ongoing maintenance and support) for autonomous vehicles and related components, as well as efforts surrounding low-cost interceptor systems. Congress would receive this report to inform its oversight of Army modernization initiatives in these technology areas.
