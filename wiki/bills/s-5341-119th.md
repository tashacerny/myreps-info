These agreements would allow the Coast Guard to obtain installation-support services—such as utilities, maintenance, security, and other base operations—from other federal, state, or local government entities.  The change would streamline the Coast Guard's ability to secure necessary services by allowing its top military officer to negotiate these agreements directly, rather than requiring approval from the department secretary. This affects Coast Guard operations and the various government agencies that might provide support services to Coast Guard installations. The bill appears designed to improve administrative efficiency in how the Coast Guard manages its facilities and services.
