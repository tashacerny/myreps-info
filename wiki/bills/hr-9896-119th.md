summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill proposes to amend the Higher Education Act of 1965 to expand what counts as \"cost of attendance\" for students pursuing higher education. Specifically, it would add certain licensure, certification, and aviation training costs to the calculation of what students are allowed to spend from federal financial aid packages. Currently, federal financial aid calculations typically include tuition, fees, room and board, and other standard education expenses. This change would broaden that definition to encompass professional licensure exams, certification programs, and aviation training—costs that students in certain fields must incur to work in their professions but that may not be directly charged by their educational institutions.  The bill would primarily affect students in fields requiring professional licenses or certifications (such as nursing, teaching, and aviation) and the institutions they attend. By including these costs in the official cost of attendance, students would be eligible to borrow or receive more federal financial aid to cover these necessary expenses. This could reduce out-of-pocket costs for students in these fields and may affect how educational institutions calculate and package financial aid awards.  The bill appears intended to address a gap in current financial aid policy where students must pay for required professional licensure and certification exams separately from their federal aid, which can create additional financial barriers to entering licensed professions."
