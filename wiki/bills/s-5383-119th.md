summary: "# Bill Summary: Scheduling of Synthetic Opioids  **Purpose and Scope** This bill would amend the Controlled Substances Act to classify three synthetic substances as Schedule I controlled drugs: MGM-15, MGM-16, and two mitragynine-related compounds (synthetic 7-hydroxymitragynine and Mitragynine Pseudoindoxyl). Schedule I is the most restrictive classification, indicating the substances have no accepted medical use and high abuse potential. The bill specifically applies a threshold requirement to the mitragynine compounds, meaning they would only be controlled above certain concentration levels. The bill also aims to expand federal enforcement authority against manufacturers and distributors of these substances, often marketed as \"gas station heroin.\"  **Who Is Affected** The bill would affect manufacturers, distributors, and retailers of these synthetic compounds; individuals who currently possess or use these substances; and federal law enforcement agencies responsible for enforcement. Healthcare providers and researchers would also be impacted if they work with these substances. State and local law enforcement may see changes in how they coordinate with federal agencies on these cases.  **Key Provisions** The bill creates new legal prohibitions on the production and distribution of the named substances while establishing enhanced enforcement mechanisms for federal agencies to pursue drug manufacturers and distributors. By setting a threshold for mitragynine compounds rather than banning them outright, the bill would allow some lower-concentration products to remain legal while targeting concentrated forms."
