summary: "# Bill Summary: Multi-Year Credit Ban Notice and Review Procedures  **Overview** This bill proposes to amend the Internal Revenue Code to modify how the IRS notifies taxpayers about and implements multi-year bans on tax credits. A multi-year ban prevents a taxpayer from claiming certain tax credits for a specified period, typically as a penalty for misusing or fraudulently claiming those credits in prior years. The bill focuses on improving the procedural requirements around these bans, particularly regarding notice to taxpayers and opportunities for review.  **Who It Affects** The bill would affect taxpayers who have been subject to multi-year credit bans or who may face such bans in the future. It could also impact the IRS's administration of tax credit programs and enforcement procedures.  **Key Changes** While the full text is unavailable, the title indicates the bill would enhance two main procedural elements: (1) the **notice requirements**—likely improving how and when the IRS informs taxpayers they are subject to a ban—and (2) the **review procedures**—potentially providing taxpayers with better opportunities to contest or appeal a ban determination. These changes would likely aim to increase transparency and ensure due process in the credit ban process."
