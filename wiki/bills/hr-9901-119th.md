summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill would overturn a final regulatory rule issued jointly by the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (USFWS) and National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) that rescinded a previous definition of \"harm\" under the Endangered Species Act (ESA). By passing this bill, Congress would reinstate the prior definition of harm that the agencies had removed through their rulemaking process.  The bill affects the regulatory framework governing protections for endangered and threatened species. The definition of \"harm\" under the ESA is significant because it determines what actions toward listed species are prohibited. It impacts federal agencies, private landowners, and businesses that conduct activities in areas where protected species live or that may affect protected species. The change also affects how USFWS and NOAA enforce ESA prohibitions.  Key to the bill is that it uses the legislative process to reverse an executive branch regulatory action. Rather than allowing the agencies' rescission rule to remain in effect, Congress would directly invalidate it through legislation, restoring the previous regulatory definition that had governed ESA harm determinations. The specific scope of impact depends on the substantive differences between the rescinded and prior definitions, which would determine how enforcement and compliance obligations change for regulated entities."
