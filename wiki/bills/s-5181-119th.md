summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill would direct the Secretary of Transportation to create a grant program focused on digitizing county roads and developing publicly accessible datasets about those roads. The program would provide funding to support counties in converting road information into digital formats and making that data available to the public. This effort aims to improve the availability and accessibility of road information at the county level.  The bill would primarily affect county governments and transportation agencies that manage local road systems. It would also benefit the public by making road data more readily available. Potential uses for such data could include infrastructure planning, maintenance coordination, emergency response, and public access to road condition information.  Key provisions would include establishing the grant program structure, defining eligibility requirements for counties seeking funding, and specifying standards for the digitized datasets to ensure they are publicly accessible and consistent across jurisdictions. The bill also indicates there are additional provisions related to these objectives, though specific details are not available from the title alone."
