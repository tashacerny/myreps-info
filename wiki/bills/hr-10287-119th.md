summary: "# Summary of the 9-8-8 Crisis Response Act  Based on the title, this bill would establish or support a three-digit crisis response number (9-8-8) analogous to the existing 911 emergency system. The 9-8-8 number would provide a dedicated hotline for individuals experiencing mental health crises, suicidal thoughts, or other behavioral health emergencies. This affects mental health providers, telecommunications companies, crisis counselors, state and local authorities responsible for emergency services, and the general public seeking mental health support.  Key provisions likely include funding for crisis call centers and trained counselors to answer 9-8-8 calls, coordination between telecommunications infrastructure and crisis services, and standards for response protocols. The bill would establish requirements for how calls are routed, answered, and handled by mental health professionals. It may also include provisions for training and staffing crisis centers, particularly in underserved areas.  The legislation aims to improve access to immediate mental health crisis support by creating a simple, memorable contact number separate from traditional police emergency services. This allows people in crisis to reach mental health specialists rather than law enforcement as a first response, potentially reducing unnecessary police involvement in behavioral health emergencies while connecting individuals to appropriate clinical resources."
