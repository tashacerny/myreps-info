summary: "# Bill Summary: Neuroblastoma Awareness Day Designation  This bill is a resolution that designates September 3, 2026, as \"Neuroblastoma Awareness Day.\" The measure recognizes and honors children and families affected by neuroblastoma, a type of cancer that develops in immature nerve cells, primarily affecting young children. The bill also acknowledges the work of medical professionals and researchers who treat patients and study this disease.  The bill affects awareness and recognition efforts related to neuroblastoma but does not create new funding, regulatory requirements, or substantive policy changes. It primarily serves a symbolic purpose by designating a specific date for public recognition and awareness-raising activities. The designation may encourage organizations, healthcare providers, and advocacy groups to use the date to educate the public about neuroblastoma, its impact on families, and ongoing research efforts to improve treatments and outcomes."
