Under the proposed changes, organizations and entities that receive certain grants through this program would be required to develop a comprehensive economic development strategy that includes measures to increase access to affordable, quality care-based services. Care-based services likely refer to healthcare, childcare, elderly care, or similar human services that support workforce participation and community wellbeing.  The bill would affect grant recipients under the Public Works and Economic Development Act—typically state agencies, local governments, and nonprofit organizations in economically distressed areas. These entities would need to incorporate care services accessibility into their economic development plans as a condition of receiving federal funding. This requirement aims to link economic development initiatives with improvements in care service availability and affordability.  The key provision requires eligible grant recipients to demonstrate how their economic development strategies will directly or indirectly improve access to affordable, quality care-based services. This could influence how organizations design projects, allocate resources, or partner with care service providers.
