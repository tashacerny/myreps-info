summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill would amend the Foreign Agents Registration Act (FARA) to extend registration requirements to social media influencers. Currently, FARA requires individuals and entities engaged in political or public relations work on behalf of foreign governments or political entities to register with the Department of Justice and disclose their activities. The proposed amendment would expand this requirement to include social media influencers who conduct such work, requiring them to file registration statements detailing their foreign client relationships and activities.  The bill would affect social media influencers, content creators, and related platforms that engage in work promoting foreign interests. It would also impact foreign governments and entities seeking to conduct influence campaigns through social media. The key provision expands FARA's disclosure requirements to capture digital influence activities that may not be covered under the current law's traditional definitions of foreign agents.  The practical effect would require affected influencers to file disclosures with the Department of Justice if they are paid by foreign actors to create content or promote messages. This aims to increase transparency regarding foreign influence efforts conducted through social media channels, allowing the government and public to identify funded advocacy on behalf of foreign interests. However, the precise scope of who qualifies as a registrable \"social media influencer\" and what activities trigger the requirement would depend on how the bill defines these terms in its full text."
