summary: "# Bill Summary  This joint resolution seeks to overturn an Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) rule through the congressional disapproval process established under the Administrative Procedure Act (chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code). Specifically, it targets the EPA's decision to grant California a waiver from federal preemption under the Clean Air Act. This waiver permits California to set its own greenhouse gas emission standards for new motor vehicles beginning with 2009 model year vehicles, rather than being required to follow federal standards.  The rule affects automobile manufacturers and the state of California. Under the Clean Air Act, California can request a waiver to establish stricter vehicle pollution controls than federal standards, and the EPA must grant this waiver unless it determines the standards are not necessary to meet air quality goals. This particular waiver allows California to implement greenhouse gas emission standards that differ from federal requirements. If Congress passes this joint resolution of disapproval, the EPA's waiver decision would be invalidated, and California would no longer be authorized to enforce its own greenhouse gas standards for those vehicles.  The bill uses the expedited \"congressional review\" process, which allows Congress to disapprove regulatory actions with a simple majority vote in both chambers and signature by the President. This procedure is designed to allow Congress to reverse agency rules relatively quickly without standard legislative procedures."
