The bill would primarily affect U.S. foreign aid policy and potentially influence funding decisions related to international health initiatives. It may also impact Gavi's operations and reach, since U.S. financial contributions are significant to the organization's work. The populations most directly affected would be children and residents of low-income countries who receive vaccines and immunization services supported by Gavi programs.  Key provisions would likely include reaffirming U.S. commitment to Gavi, potentially authorizing or appropriating funds for continued American contributions to the organization, and supporting Gavi's mission to increase immunization coverage in developing nations.
