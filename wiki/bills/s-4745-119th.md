The bill is a naming designation—it does not authorize new funding, establish new programs, or make operational changes to the postal facility.  The bill affects the U.S. Postal Service facility in Georgetown, Ohio, and would apply the honorary name to that location. Georgetown is the birthplace of Ulysses S. Grant, the 18th U.S. President and Civil War general, making the geographic connection relevant to the naming proposal. This type of legislation is procedural in nature and requires Congressional action to officially designate federal buildings with commemorative names."
