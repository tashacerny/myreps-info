summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill proposes to designate two historic sites in Mississippi as affiliated areas of the National Park System: the Grand Village of the Natchez Indians and Jefferson College. Affiliated areas are historic or cultural sites that meet national significance standards but are not directly operated by the National Park Service. Instead, they typically remain under local or state management while receiving National Park System recognition and access to certain federal support programs.  The bill affects the National Park System's administrative structure and may impact tourism, educational programming, and preservation funding for these two locations. The Grand Village of the Natchez Indians is an archaeological site representing the pre-contact and early colonial period settlement of the Natchez people. Jefferson College is a historic educational institution. By establishing them as affiliated areas, the bill would allow these sites to be officially recognized as part of the National Park System framework, potentially enhancing their visibility and eligibility for federal preservation grants and technical assistance.
