summary: "# Summary: Sunset Section 230 Act  **What the Bill Proposes** Based on the title, this bill would establish a sunset provision—an automatic expiration date—for Section 230 of the Communications Decency Act of 1996. Section 230 is a federal law that provides broad liability protections to online platforms and websites, generally shielding them from legal responsibility for content posted by users. A sunset provision would cause this legal protection to expire on a set date unless Congress votes to renew it.  **Who It Affects** This would primarily affect online platforms, social media companies, and websites that host user-generated content. It could also affect users, advertisers, and anyone who publishes content online, as platforms might change their moderation practices, policies, or services in response to increased legal liability. The changes could also affect individuals and organizations that might pursue legal claims against platforms for hosted content.  **Key Implications** If Section 230 protections expire, platforms would face potential liability for content posted by their users, which could lead to changes in how companies moderate content, the services they offer, or the costs associated with operating online platforms. Congress would need to act before the sunset date to maintain the current legal framework or could allow the expiration to proceed, fundamentally reshaping liability rules for online services.  --- *Note: Without access to the full bill text, specific details about the proposed sunset date and any other provisions cannot be confirmed.*"
