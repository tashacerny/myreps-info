summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill proposes to amend the Foreign Service Act of 1980 to expand per diem benefits for Foreign Service members. Specifically, it would make Foreign Service members eligible to receive per diem (daily allowances for meals and incidental expenses) while taking required leave. Currently, per diem eligibility appears to be limited to official travel and duty assignments, and this change would extend those benefits to cover periods when members are mandated to take leave.  The bill affects members of the Foreign Service—including diplomats, consular officers, and other career federal employees assigned to work at U.S. embassies, consulates, and diplomatic missions abroad. It would apply to situations where leave is required rather than voluntary, such as rest-and-recuperation leave policies that some overseas positions mandate.  The key provision is the expansion of per diem eligibility during required leave periods. This would likely increase compensation or reimbursement for Foreign Service members during these mandated absences from their duty stations, potentially making overseas assignments more financially sustainable for career foreign service employees."
