summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill would prevent the Secretary of the Interior from authorizing any expansion of the Mid-Continent Limestone Quarry near Glenwood Springs, Colorado. The legislation appears designed to restrict federal actions that would allow the quarry operation to increase in size or scope. The bill would directly affect the quarry operator, the Department of the Interior, and potentially local communities near Glenwood Springs.  The main provision prohibits the Secretary of the Interior from taking action—which could include issuing permits, approvals, leases, or other authorizations—to expand the quarry. This would apply to any federal decisions or permits under the Interior Department's jurisdiction. The measure affects how federal land management and permitting decisions are made regarding this specific mining operation.  Those potentially impacted include the quarry operator (who would be unable to obtain federal approval for expansion), the Department of the Interior (which would be restricted in its discretionary authority), and local stakeholders in the Glenwood Springs area. The bill does not address the quarry's current operations, only future expansion."
