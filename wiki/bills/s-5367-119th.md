summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill would modify federal law regarding payments made to military service members who leave the Armed Forces. Specifically, it addresses three types of separation payments: separation pay, special separation benefits, and voluntary separation incentive payments. Currently, the Department of Defense can recoup (recover) these payments from service members if they later become eligible for Department of Veterans Affairs disability compensation. The bill would eliminate this recoupment requirement entirely when service members receive VA disability compensation.  The bill also restricts the Department of Defense's authority to recoup separation payments from service members who subsequently receive military retirement or retainer pay. Rather than allowing automatic recoupment in these cases, the bill would impose new limitations on when and how the Secretary of Defense can recover such payments.  In effect, the bill would allow military members who receive separation payments to retain those funds even if they later qualify for VA disability benefits, and would limit recoupment in cases where service members become eligible for military retirement or retainer pay. The changes affect former service members transitioning out of the Armed Forces and their financial security after separation."
