summary: "# Bill Summary  This resolution honors small manufacturers of firearms and their contributions to the U.S. economy, culture, and recreational heritage. The resolution designates August 2026 as \"National Shooting Sports Month.\" As a resolution, this measure expresses the sense of Congress but does not create law or impose regulatory requirements.  The resolution recognizes small firearms manufacturers as contributors to the economy and identifies shooting sports as part of American recreational and cultural traditions. It affects no specific groups through regulatory action, though it acknowledges the firearms industry and shooting sports communities. The designation of a national observance month is ceremonial in nature.  Key provisions include the formal recognition of small firearms manufacturers' economic and cultural role and the establishment of August 2026 as the designated month for national recognition of shooting sports. The resolution is non-binding and does not allocate funding, establish new programs, or modify existing law."
