summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill proposes amendments to Medicaid (Title XIX of the Social Security Act) to expand coverage and access to home and community-based services. Rather than requiring institutional care, the legislation would make it easier for Medicaid beneficiaries to receive services in their homes and communities. The bill also establishes grant programs to support the direct care workforce—including recruitment, training, education, retention, and career advancement for workers who provide these services. Additionally, it creates grants to help support family members who serve as caregivers.  The bill affects multiple groups: Medicaid beneficiaries (particularly elderly individuals and people with disabilities), direct care workers such as home health aides and personal care attendants, family caregivers, and state Medicaid programs. By expanding home and community-based services, the legislation aims to give beneficiaries more options beyond institutional settings while addressing workforce challenges in the direct care field.  Key provisions include mandatory Medicaid coverage for expanded home and community-based services, dedicated funding for workforce development initiatives, and support programs for family caregivers. The grants component targets improving recruitment, training, and retention in direct care occupations—areas the healthcare sector has identified as facing significant staffing challenges."
