summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill would prohibit the use of federal funds to pay for gender transition procedures or treatments for individuals under 18 years of age. The restriction would apply across federal programs and agencies that distribute taxpayer money, including Medicare, Medicaid, the Veterans Health Administration, and the Department of Defense. The bill affects minors who might otherwise receive such care through federally funded health insurance or medical programs.  Key provisions would likely include a ban on federal funding for medical interventions related to gender transition—such as hormone therapy, surgical procedures, and related mental health services—for patients younger than 18. The bill would not directly restrict minors' access to these treatments if paid through private insurance or out-of-pocket, nor would it necessarily affect care funded entirely through state or local resources. Federal healthcare providers and programs receiving federal funds would need to comply with these restrictions or face potential loss of funding."
