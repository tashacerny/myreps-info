summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill proposes to officially designate the outdoor amphitheater at the Blue Ridge Music Center in Galax, Virginia, with a commemorative name: the \"Rick Boucher Amphitheater.\" The Blue Ridge Music Center is a cultural venue in southwestern Virginia that hosts musical performances and events. This action would rename the facility's outdoor amphitheater in honor of Rick Boucher.  The bill affects the Blue Ridge Music Center and how its amphitheater is officially identified and referred to in the future. It is a commemorative measure that does not typically involve funding, operational changes, or direct impact on other parties. The designation would recognize Boucher's connection to the region or contributions deemed significant enough to warrant this honor at a public cultural institution."
