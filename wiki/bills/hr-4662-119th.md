summary: "# Summary of Paul Ignatius Post Office Designation Bill  This bill proposes to rename a specific United States Postal Service facility located at 6444 San Fernando Road in Glendale, California. The facility would be officially designated as the \"Paul Ignatius Post Office.\" This type of legislation is procedural in nature and does not change the operations, funding, or services of the post office—it simply assigns an official name to the building.  The bill affects the postal facility in Glendale, California, and those who use its services. Post office facility designations are typically named in honor of individuals with significant historical, military, or public service records. The naming would be reflected in official postal records and signage at the location."
