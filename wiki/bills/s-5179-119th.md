summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill proposes to modify how Title IX of the Education Amendments of 1972—the federal law prohibiting sex-based discrimination in education—is interpreted and applied. Specifically, the bill would clarify that Title IX's protections against sex discrimination are based on \"biological\" sex rather than other understandings of sex or gender identity. The bill would apply to all educational institutions and programs that receive federal financial assistance, which includes most public schools, colleges, and universities, as well as many private institutions.  The bill would affect students, staff, and administrators at federally-funded educational institutions. Key provisions would include establishing that sex-based discrimination protections under Title IX are defined according to biological sex. This change would have implications for policies and practices in areas such as student housing, athletics, and other sex-separated facilities or programs. The bill would require educational institutions receiving federal funds to align their nondiscrimination policies with this definition.  The bill represents a significant shift in how federal nondiscrimination law in education is interpreted, narrowing the scope of what constitutes sex-based discrimination under Title IX compared to some recent regulatory guidance that has included gender identity protections under the statute."
