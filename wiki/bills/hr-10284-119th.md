summary: "# Smart Meter Data Privacy Protection Act — Summary  Based on the bill title, this legislation would establish privacy protections for data collected by smart meters—electronic devices that measure and transmit electricity, gas, or water consumption. The bill would likely apply to utility companies, meter manufacturers, and potentially third parties that access meter data. It would affect residential and commercial consumers whose consumption patterns are tracked through these devices.  Key provisions would probably include requirements around data collection, use, and sharing practices. These might involve obtaining customer consent before sharing data, limiting how utilities can use consumption information, setting security standards to prevent unauthorized access, and establishing procedures for customers to access or correct their own data. The bill may also define penalties for violations and create oversight mechanisms.  The underlying concern addressed by this legislation is that detailed consumption data—which reveals patterns about when and how much energy or water people use—could potentially be misused if not properly protected, sold to third parties, or accessed by unauthorized parties. Smart meter privacy bills typically aim to balance utility companies' operational needs with consumer privacy interests."
