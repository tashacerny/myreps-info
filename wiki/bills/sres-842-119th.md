summary: "# Summary of National Hotel Employee Day Resolution  This resolution expresses recognition and appreciation for the contributions made by hotel employees in the United States. The measure supports the designation and goals of National Hotel Employee Day, which is intended to highlight the work performed by individuals employed in the hotel and hospitality industry.  The resolution affects hotel workers across the country, including housekeeping staff, front desk personnel, maintenance workers, food service employees, and other positions within hotels and lodging establishments. By supporting this observance, the resolution acknowledges the role these workers play in the hospitality sector and the broader economy.  As a resolution, this measure does not create new laws or require government action beyond expressing Congressional recognition. It is a symbolic statement of support for elevating awareness of hotel employee contributions and celebrating their work in the industry."
