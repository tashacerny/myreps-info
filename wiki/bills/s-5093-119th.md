summary: "# Bill Summary: Office of the Ombudsperson for Immigrant Children in Immigration Custody  **What the Bill Proposes**  This bill would create a new federal office called the Office of the Ombudsperson for Immigrant Children in Immigration Custody. An ombudsperson is an independent official authorized to investigate complaints and advocate on behalf of individuals who believe they have been treated unfairly by government agencies. In this case, the office would be responsible for overseeing the treatment of children who are in the custody of U.S. immigration authorities.  **Who It Affects**  The primary group affected would be immigrant children (minors under 18) who are detained or held in custody by immigration enforcement agencies, such as U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) or the Department of Homeland Security. The office would also affect the government agencies responsible for managing these children's care and the conditions of their detention.  **Key Provisions**  While the full text is unavailable, the office would likely be authorized to receive and investigate complaints from detained children or their representatives regarding their treatment, conditions of confinement, and access to services. The ombudsperson would typically have powers to conduct inspections, request documents and information from immigration agencies, and issue reports with recommendations for improving conditions or practices."
