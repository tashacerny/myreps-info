summary: "# Bill Summary  This resolution recognizes the 1960 University of Missouri Tigers football team for completing an undefeated regular season, winning the Orange Bowl, and claiming the 1960 National Championship. The resolution is a commemorative measure that honors the achievements of the team during that season. It does not create new laws or establish binding requirements; rather, it formally acknowledges a historical athletic accomplishment by the Missouri Tigers.  The resolution affects the University of Missouri and members of the 1960 football team, as well as the broader Missouri community. It serves as an official state recognition of the team's performance during that specific season. By passing this resolution, the legislature expresses formal recognition of the team's accomplishments and their significance to the state's history."
