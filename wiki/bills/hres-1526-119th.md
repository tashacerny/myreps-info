summary: "# Bill Summary: Patriot Week Designation  This bill is a commemorative resolution expressing support for designating the week of September 11–17, 2026, as \"Patriot Week.\" As a resolution rather than legislation that creates law, this measure serves to formally recognize and encourage observance of this week, rather than to establish binding requirements or allocate funding.  The designation would affect the general public, schools, government agencies, and organizations that choose to participate in commemorative activities during that week. The timing coincides with the anniversary of the September 11, 2001 terrorist attacks, suggesting the week is intended to honor those affected by those events and promote patriotic reflection or community engagement during this period.  The bill itself contains no substantive provisions beyond the expression of support for the designation. It does not mandate activities, establish programs, appropriate funds, or create legal obligations. Participation in recognizing \"Patriot Week\" would be voluntary."
