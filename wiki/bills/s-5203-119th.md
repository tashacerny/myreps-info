summary: "# Bill Summary: Exempt Facility Bonds Amendment  **What the Bill Proposes**  This bill would modify federal tax rules governing exempt facility bonds, which are municipal bonds issued to finance specific types of infrastructure and facilities. The bonds currently allow investors to earn interest income free from federal income taxation, making them an attractive financing tool for qualifying projects. The bill would adjust the rules that determine which facilities and projects are eligible for this tax-exempt bond status.  **Who It Affects**  The changes would impact several groups: state and local governments that issue these bonds to fund infrastructure projects; investors who purchase the bonds; and the entities that operate the funded facilities (such as airports, toll roads, water systems, or energy facilities). Federal tax revenues could be affected depending on which facilities become more or less eligible for tax-exempt financing.  **Key Provisions**  While the full text is unavailable, bills of this type typically modify provisions such as: eligibility criteria for what types of facilities qualify; definitions of \"exempt facilities\"; or rules governing bond issuance and use of proceeds. The specific changes would determine whether certain projects become easier or harder to finance through tax-exempt bonds, and whether the benefit becomes more or less widely available."
