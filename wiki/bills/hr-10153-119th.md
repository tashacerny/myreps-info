summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill would amend the Internal Revenue Code to classify income earned by U.S. merchant mariners as \"foreign earned income.\" Merchant mariners are seafaring workers employed on U.S.-flagged commercial vessels. Under current tax law, foreign earned income typically qualifies for the Foreign Earned Income Exclusion (FEIE), which allows U.S. citizens working abroad to exclude a portion of their earnings from federal income taxation. This bill would extend that tax treatment to merchant mariners working on ships.  The primary effect would be to reduce federal income taxes owed by merchant mariners by allowing them to exclude a portion of their earnings from taxable income, similar to other U.S. workers employed overseas. This would affect merchant mariners employed on U.S. commercial vessels and could reduce federal tax revenue. The change would apply to maritime workers who meet the definition of merchant mariners under the bill's provisions.  The key provision is the reclassification of merchant mariner income as foreign earned income for tax purposes, making these workers eligible for the same income exclusions currently available to other U.S. workers abroad."
