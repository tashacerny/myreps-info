The legislation would increase the fund's ability to borrow money on a temporary basis and modify how money must be distributed to eligible victims.  The bill primarily affects U.S. citizens and nationals who are eligible claimants under the terrorism compensation program, as well as the fund's operations and finances. By expanding borrowing authority, the bill would allow the fund to access additional resources to meet payment obligations.
