summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill aims to establish or support national training, technical assistance, and resource centers focused on augmentative and alternative communication (AAC). AAC refers to methods and tools that help individuals with severe expressive communication disabilities—those who have difficulty speaking or being understood—communicate with others. These may include devices, software, sign language interpretation, or other assistive technologies.  The bill's primary goal is to ensure that all individuals with significant expressive communication disabilities have access to the AAC tools and support they need. By funding training and technical assistance centers, the legislation intends to expand knowledge and capacity among service providers, educators, healthcare professionals, and others who work with this population. This increased support infrastructure would help beneficiaries participate more fully in education, employment, social activities, and other aspects of daily life.  The bill affects individuals with expressive communication disabilities, as well as service providers, educators, and institutions that support them. Key provisions likely include funding mechanisms for the centers themselves and specifications regarding what training and technical assistance they should provide, though the full text would detail the scope, budget, and implementation requirements."
