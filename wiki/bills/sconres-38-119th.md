summary: "# Summary of Concurrent Resolution on FY 2027 Budget  This concurrent resolution establishes the federal budget framework for fiscal year 2027 and sets budgetary targets for the subsequent nine fiscal years through 2036. A concurrent budget resolution is a blueprint that Congress uses to guide spending and revenue decisions; it does not become law but instead serves as a procedural tool to coordinate budget-related legislation. The resolution would affect all federal agencies, programs, and activities that rely on appropriations or other federal funding.  Key aspects of a budget resolution of this scope typically include: overall spending limits (budget authority); revenue projections; deficit or surplus targets; allocations among major budget categories such as defense, healthcare, and infrastructure; and limits for different congressional committees as they develop specific appropriations and tax bills. By setting multi-year targets through 2036, the resolution provides a long-term fiscal framework intended to guide lawmakers' decisions on spending priorities and tax policy over a decade.  The resolution affects federal agencies, federal employees, benefit recipients, and taxpayers—essentially all parties dependent on or contributing to the federal budget. The specific impacts depend on the actual spending and revenue levels set within the resolution, which the summary title does not detail. Congress uses budget resolutions to manage the overall fiscal direction of government operations and finances."
