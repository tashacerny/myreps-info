summary: "# Small Unmanned Aircraft Systems Operational Test, Evaluation, and Training Corridor Act  **Overview and Purpose**  This bill establishes a designated corridor or corridors for testing, evaluating, and training operations involving small unmanned aircraft systems (commonly known as drones). The legislation aims to create controlled airspace where government agencies, private companies, and researchers can conduct research and development activities with unmanned aircraft under structured conditions, rather than dispersing these operations across various locations.  **Key Provisions and Affected Parties**  The bill would affect federal aviation authorities (primarily the Federal Aviation Administration), drone manufacturers, research institutions, and private operators conducting unmanned aircraft testing. The corridor(s) would provide a defined geographic area with established safety protocols, regulatory oversight, and operational guidelines. This allows stakeholders to conduct flight tests, gather performance data, and train personnel in a coordinated environment while maintaining separation from general aviation and populated areas.  **Purpose and Impact**  By consolidating testing activities in designated corridors, the legislation seeks to advance the development and integration of unmanned aircraft technology, reduce regulatory barriers to innovation, and streamline the approval process for new drone operations. This approach benefits technology development while maintaining safety standards and airspace management. The corridor concept also allows the FAA and other agencies to monitor and evaluate how unmanned systems operate before broader commercial deployment."
