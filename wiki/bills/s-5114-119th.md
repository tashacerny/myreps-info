summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill would modify the Federal Transit Administration's Fixed Guideway Capital Investment Grants (CIG) program by expanding the methods used to forecast ridership for proposed transit projects. The CIG program provides federal funding for capital projects like rail systems, bus rapid transit lines, and other fixed guideway transit infrastructure. Currently, the program uses specific forecasting methodologies to evaluate whether projects are justified and merit federal investment. This bill would allow additional ridership forecasting methods to be considered in these project justification determinations.  The bill primarily affects transit agencies seeking federal funding, the Federal Transit Administration, and communities considering new fixed guideway transit projects. By broadening acceptable forecasting approaches, the legislation could influence which projects receive federal support and potentially make it easier for projects using alternative forecasting methods to qualify for grants.  Key effects would include: (1) expanding the toolkit of forecasting methods available to evaluate transit projects; (2) potentially affecting project selection and approval processes under the CIG program; and (3) allowing more flexibility in how project sponsors demonstrate ridership projections. The bill's practical impact would depend on which specific forecasting methods are included and how substantially they differ from current approved approaches."
