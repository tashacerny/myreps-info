summary: "# Summary of Ethics and Conduct Bill for Federal Judges  This bill proposes to establish formal ethics standards for justices and judges serving in U.S. federal courts. Currently, federal judges are not bound by the same Code of Conduct that applies to other federal employees, though the Judicial Conference has adopted a voluntary ethics code. The bill would amend federal law to create a mandatory code of conduct applicable to all federal justices and judges, standardizing ethical expectations across the judiciary.  The bill creates an \"Ethics Investigations Counsel\" position to handle investigations into potential ethics violations by federal judges. This new office would presumably review complaints and conduct inquiries into judicial misconduct, adding an independent oversight mechanism to the current system where the Judicial Conference handles ethics matters. This change affects all federal judges and justices, including Supreme Court justices.  Additionally, the bill requires federal judges to publicly disclose when they recuse themselves from cases and the reasons for doing so. Currently, judges may recuse themselves without detailed public explanation. This transparency requirement would provide litigants and the public with information about judicial recusals. Together, these provisions aim to increase ethics accountability, transparency, and public confidence in the federal judicial system."
