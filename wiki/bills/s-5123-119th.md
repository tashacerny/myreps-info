summary: "# Summary of Lunar Landing Day Bill  This bill proposes to amend federal law to establish July 20 as an official national observance called Lunar Landing Day. The date commemorates the Apollo 11 moon landing, when astronauts first landed on the lunar surface on July 20, 1969. By designating this date in the U.S. Code, Congress would formally recognize the historical significance of this space exploration milestone.  The bill affects all Americans, as it creates a national day of observance that can be used to honor and celebrate the achievement of the Apollo program and the astronauts involved. Government offices, schools, and organizations may use the designation to hold educational events or ceremonies marking the occasion. The designation does not appear to require federal employees to have the day off or create mandatory closures, as that authority typically comes through separate legislation.  The primary provision is the amendment to title 36 of the United States Code (which contains federal law on patriotic and national observances) to officially list July 20 as Lunar Landing Day. This is a symbolic designation that formally recognizes the historical event in federal law."
