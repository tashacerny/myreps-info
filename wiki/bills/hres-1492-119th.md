summary: "# Summary of Pacific Islands Forum Partnership Resolution  This resolution expresses Congressional support for the United States' partnership with the Pacific Islands Forum (PIF) and its 18 member nations. The measure affirms commitment to ongoing cooperation with these island nations and signals that the U.S. intends to maintain active engagement in the Pacific region. The resolution does not establish new programs or funding; rather, it is a statement of Congressional intent regarding U.S. foreign policy priorities in the Pacific.  The resolution affects U.S. relations with Pacific island nations, including countries such as Fiji, Papua New Guinea, Samoa, Solomon Islands, and others in the region. It also reflects broader Congressional priorities regarding U.S. involvement in Pacific regional affairs and stability in the area.  Key themes indicated by the title include commitment to regional stability, economic prosperity, and peaceful relations. The resolution characterizes the desired U.S. role as \"strong, reliable, and active,\" suggesting an emphasis on consistent diplomatic and strategic engagement in what is referred to as the \"Blue Pacific\" (a regional development framework). As a resolution expressing the \"sense of the House,\" this measure conveys Congressional sentiment rather than creating binding legal requirements or new appropriations."
