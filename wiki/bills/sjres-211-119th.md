summary: "# Bill Summary  This joint resolution would direct the removal of U.S. Armed Forces from any military hostilities involving Iran that have not been explicitly authorized by Congress. The measure asserts congressional authority over military operations and seeks to ensure that any armed conflict with Iran must receive prior congressional approval rather than proceeding under existing statutory authorizations or executive authority alone.  The resolution affects the Department of Defense and any U.S. military personnel or operations that might be engaged in hostilities with Iran. It also touches on the balance of power between the executive and legislative branches regarding war powers.  Key provisions would likely include a directive requiring the President to cease military operations against Iran (unless already authorized by Congress) and possibly establish a timeline for withdrawal. Such resolutions typically invoke the War Powers Resolution of 1973, which requires the President to notify Congress of military actions and limits their duration without congressional approval. This bill appears designed to clarify that any future military action against Iran requires explicit new authorization from Congress rather than relying on older authorizations related to other conflicts."
