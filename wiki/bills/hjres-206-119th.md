summary: "# Summary  This bill proposes a constitutional amendment that would establish a requirement for members of Congress to be natural born citizens of the United States. Currently, the Constitution requires the President to be a natural born citizen, but it only requires Representatives and Senators to be U.S. citizens (without specifying natural born status). This amendment would extend the natural born citizen requirement to all members of the House of Representatives and Senate.  The amendment would affect anyone seeking election to Congress. Natural born citizenship generally refers to citizenship obtained at birth through U.S. citizenship of one or both parents or birth on U.S. soil, as opposed to citizenship obtained through naturalization later in life. If ratified, this change would make individuals who became citizens through naturalization ineligible to serve in Congress, even if they meet all other constitutional requirements.  The proposal would require approval by a two-thirds majority in both the House and Senate, followed by ratification by at least 38 state legislatures to become part of the Constitution."
