summary: "# Summary: Constitutional Amendment on Supreme Court Size  This proposed constitutional amendment would establish that the U.S. Supreme Court must consist of exactly nine justices. Currently, the number of justices is set by federal statute (28 U.S.C. § 1), which Congress can change through legislation. This amendment would place the nine-justice requirement directly in the Constitution, making it much more difficult to alter—any change would require a new constitutional amendment rather than a simple act of Congress.  The amendment would affect the Supreme Court's composition and any future efforts to expand or reduce the number of justices. It would apply to Congress (which currently has the power to set Court size) and would constrain the judiciary's structure going forward. The provision would be binding on all future Congresses and would require supermajority support (two-thirds of both chambers) to propose any alternative number and three-fourths of state legislatures to ratify.  Key provisions include codifying the nine-justice standard in the Constitution itself, effectively removing this matter from the ordinary legislative process. This prevents either major party or future Congresses from unilaterally changing the Court's size without undertaking the rigorous amendment process."
