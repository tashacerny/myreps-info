summary: "# Bill Summary: Tourette Syndrome Awareness Day Recognition  This bill expresses congressional support for designating June 7, 2026, as \"Tourette Syndrome Awareness Day.\" The measure is a ceremonial resolution intended to recognize and raise awareness about Tourette syndrome, a neurological condition characterized by involuntary repetitive movements and vocalizations called tics. The bill does not create new programs, funding, or legal requirements.  The designation would primarily affect advocacy organizations, medical professionals, and educational institutions focused on Tourette syndrome, who could use the official recognition to promote public understanding of the condition. It may also benefit individuals with Tourette syndrome and their families by increasing visibility of the disorder.  Key features of the bill include the specific date chosen (June 7, 2026) and the symbolic nature of the recognition. As a resolution expressing support rather than creating enforceable policy, the bill carries no direct fiscal impact or regulatory consequences. Its primary purpose is to encourage awareness and understanding of Tourette syndrome among the general public and policymakers."
