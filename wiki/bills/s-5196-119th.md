summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill would restrict the Department of Justice (DOJ) from granting immunity to the President, Vice President, or their close relatives and associated entities in connection with certain crimes. The measure aims to establish a legal barrier preventing the DOJ from offering immunity agreements—which typically involve reduced charges or sentences in exchange for testimony or cooperation—to these individuals and related parties. The bill would directly affect sitting and former Presidents and Vice Presidents, as well as their immediate family members, family trusts, parents, siblings, and business entities affiliated with or owned by these officials.  The bill's key provisions include a prohibition on DOJ immunity grants to the specified individuals and entities, though the full text is not available to detail any exceptions, definitions of crimes covered, effective dates, or enforcement mechanisms. The scope of \"related companies or affiliates\" would need clarification in the full legislative text to understand exactly which business entities would be covered. The bill does not appear to restrict other prosecutorial tools or plea agreements; it specifically targets immunity arrangements."
