summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill would require health insurance plans to cover testing for certain sexually transmitted infections (STIs) without charging patients out-of-pocket costs such as copayments, coinsurance, or deductibles. The requirement would apply to preventive STI testing services, meaning patients could access these tests at no direct cost when using covered health plans.  The bill affects health insurance providers and their enrollees. It would apply to individuals covered by health insurance plans, expanding existing preventive care protections to ensure STI testing is accessible without financial barriers. The specific STIs covered and the scope of testing (such as frequency or age groups) would be defined in the bill's provisions.  By removing cost-sharing requirements, the bill aims to reduce financial obstacles to STI screening and early detection, which public health authorities generally recommend as a preventive care measure. The measure would align STI testing with similar preventive health services that many insurance plans already cover without cost sharing."
