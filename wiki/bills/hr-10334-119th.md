summary: "# Bill Summary: Electronic Filing Identification Number Validation  This bill would require the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) to establish and implement a validation system for electronic filing identification (EFIN) numbers before they can be used to electronically submit tax returns and other tax documents. EFINs are unique identifiers assigned to tax professionals, firms, and authorized e-file providers that allow them to submit tax documents to the IRS electronically. The proposed validation requirement would create a verification process to confirm the identity and legitimacy of users before granting or maintaining EFIN access.  The bill would primarily affect tax professionals, preparers, and e-file service providers who currently use EFINs to submit returns on behalf of clients. It may also impact tax filing businesses and accounting firms that rely on electronic filing capabilities. The IRS would bear responsibility for implementing and administering the new validation system.  The key provisions would likely include establishing validation procedures to verify EFIN holders' identities, credentials, and eligibility; implementing periodic re-validation requirements; and potentially adding security measures to reduce fraudulent use of EFINs. Such measures could help prevent identity theft, reduce fraudulent tax filing, and improve data security in the electronic filing system."
