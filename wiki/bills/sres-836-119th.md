summary: "# Summary of National Airborne Day Resolution  This resolution proposes to designate August 16, 2026, as \"National Airborne Day\" in the United States. The date commemorates the establishment of the airborne forces, marking a specific historical milestone in military aviation. The resolution is a ceremonial measure that recognizes and honors this military tradition on a designated annual date.  The resolution affects the general public, military personnel—particularly those serving in airborne units—and federal recognition of military heritage. National recognition days do not typically create legal obligations or appropriations but serve to officially acknowledge the date as significant at the federal level. Such designations are commonly used to raise public awareness of historical events and honor specific groups or traditions.  If enacted, this resolution would establish August 16, 2026, as an officially recognized observance day, allowing for commemorative events and public acknowledgment of airborne military forces and their historical contributions."
