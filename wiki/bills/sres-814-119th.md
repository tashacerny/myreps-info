summary: "# Bill Summary  This resolution recognizes the significance of the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) of 1990 in enabling individuals with disabilities to achieve independent living and economic self-sufficiency. The resolution emphasizes the importance of protecting the rights of people with disabilities to live in their own homes and participate in their communities, rather than being institutionalized or relegated to segregated settings. The measure does not create new laws or programs but instead affirms the value and necessity of existing disability rights protections.  The resolution affects individuals with disabilities and organizations that serve them, including disability rights advocates, healthcare providers, and state and local governments responsible for implementing disability services. It also speaks to Congress's commitment to disability rights policy more broadly.  Key aspects of the resolution include recognition of the ADA's role in promoting independence and self-sufficiency for people with disabilities, and a call to protect and maintain the right of individuals with disabilities to live in integrated community settings of their choosing. As a resolution rather than legislation, this measure expresses the intent of Congress but does not enact binding requirements or allocate funding."
