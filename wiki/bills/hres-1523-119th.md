summary: "# Bill Summary: Carbon Monoxide Poisoning Awareness Day  This bill is a commemorative measure that expresses congressional support for designating November 20, 2026, as \"Carbon Monoxide Poisoning Awareness Day.\" The bill does not create new laws or establish federal programs. Rather, it is a symbolic resolution intended to raise public awareness about carbon monoxide poisoning, a health hazard caused by exposure to the colorless, odorless gas produced by engines, appliances, and heating systems.  The designation would affect public health messaging and awareness efforts, potentially prompting government agencies, health organizations, and the private sector to conduct educational campaigns on that date. The day could be used to inform the public about carbon monoxide risks, prevention measures (such as proper ventilation and detector installation), and recognizing symptoms of poisoning.  This type of bill typically has no direct fiscal impact or regulatory effect. Its primary purpose is to draw attention to a public health issue and encourage Americans to take preventive measures against accidental carbon monoxide exposure, which can cause serious injury or death if undetected."
