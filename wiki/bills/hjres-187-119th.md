summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill uses the Congressional Review Act (CRA) to disapprove a rule issued by the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) regarding prior authorization requirements under the Medicare WISeR (Wasteful and Inappropriate Services Reduction) Model. The WISeR Model is a CMS initiative designed to reduce unnecessary or inappropriate medical services in Medicare by requiring advance approval before certain services are delivered. If passed, this bill would invalidate the rule and prevent CMS from implementing substantially similar rules without new congressional authorization.  The rule affects Medicare providers, beneficiaries, and CMS's ability to manage program spending. Providers would be subject to prior authorization requirements for select services under the model, while Medicare beneficiaries could experience delays in receiving certain treatments pending approval. The bill directly impacts how CMS implements cost-control measures within the Medicare program.  The key provision is the formal disapproval of the WISeR Model prior authorization rule under the CRA process. The CRA allows Congress to overturn federal regulations through a simplified legislative process. If enacted, the bill would eliminate the prior authorization requirements outlined in the rule and constrain CMS's authority to implement similar approval mechanisms without separate legislative action."
