The measure would establish a uniform standard across all states for voter identity verification at polling places. The bill affects all voters participating in federal elections and potentially state and local elections, depending on implementation details.  Key provisions would likely include defining what types of photo identification are acceptable (such as driver's licenses, passports, or state-issued ID cards) and establishing procedures for voters whose identification is unavailable or rejected. The bill would also presumably address how states enforce the requirement and what recourse or provisional voting options exist for voters unable to produce acceptable identification.  The measure impacts election administration at the state and local level, as it would require changes to polling place procedures and potentially voter registration systems. It would also affect various voter populations differently, depending on how broadly acceptable forms of identification are defined and what alternative procedures are available for voters without photo ID."
