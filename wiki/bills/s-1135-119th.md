summary: "# Bill Summary: Bonneville Shoreline Trail Feasibility Study  This bill would amend the National Trails System Act to require the Secretary of the Interior to study whether the Bonneville Shoreline Trail should be formally designated as part of the National Trails System. The Bonneville Shoreline Trail is a hiking and recreation trail located in Utah that generally follows the ancient shoreline of prehistoric Lake Bonneville. The feasibility study would assess whether the trail meets the criteria for national designation.  The study would likely examine factors such as the trail's historical and recreational significance, current conditions, land ownership and access issues, and whether designation would be compatible with existing land uses. The bill would affect the Secretary of the Interior and relevant federal agencies responsible for implementing the study, as well as landowners and local communities along the proposed trail route whose land or interests could be involved.  A formal designation under the National Trails System Act would provide the trail with federal recognition and could make it eligible for certain federal funding and management support, though the study itself does not authorize designation—it only directs an examination of feasibility for potential future action."
