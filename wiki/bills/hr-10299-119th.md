summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill proposes to designate a specific U.S. Postal Service facility in Alamo, Tennessee as the \"Kenneth Nolen Davis Post Office.\" The facility is located at 83 East Park Street. Designating post offices after individuals is a common congressional practice that honors the person's name by associating it with the building.  The bill affects the postal facility in Alamo and any individuals or organizations connected to Kenneth Nolen Davis, for whom the post office would be named. The key provision is simply the official renaming of the postal facility. No changes to postal operations, funding, or services are implied by this designation. Post office naming bills are typically noncontroversial legislative measures that require no funding and serve a commemorative purpose."
