summary: "# Summary of the Expedited Access to Biosimilars Act  **What the bill proposes:** This bill would establish a faster regulatory pathway for biosimilar drugs to receive approval and reach the market. Biosimilars are medications designed to be highly similar to existing biologic drugs (complex drugs made from living organisms). The legislation aims to streamline the approval process, reducing the time and regulatory requirements that biosimilar manufacturers must meet before these drugs can be sold to patients.  **Who it affects:** The bill would primarily affect pharmaceutical manufacturers that produce biosimilars, the FDA (which reviews drug applications), and patients who use biologic medications. Potentially, it could also affect the manufacturers of original biologic drugs and healthcare systems that purchase medications.  **Key provisions (based on title):** The bill's core purpose is to expedite biosimilar access, which likely includes measures such as reducing the data requirements for approval, shortening review timelines, or creating a streamlined FDA review track. The general intent would be to bring lower-cost biosimilar alternatives to market more quickly, potentially increasing competition with original biologics and improving patient access to more affordable treatment options.  **Note:** A detailed summary of specific provisions would require access to the full bill text."
