summary: "# Bill Summary: Clean Air Act Grants for Air Sensors  **Overview and Purpose**  This bill clarifies how existing grant programs under the Clean Air Act can be used to purchase air quality monitoring equipment, specifically air sensors. The legislation addresses the use of funds already authorized under the Clean Air Act rather than creating new funding mechanisms.  **Key Provisions and Affected Parties**  The bill clarifies that certain existing grants—likely those administered by the Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) and distributed to states, tribes, and local air quality agencies—may be used to acquire air sensing technology. This affects states, municipalities, tribal governments, and other entities responsible for monitoring and reporting air quality. Air sensors can provide real-time or near-real-time data on pollutant concentrations, potentially supplementing or replacing more traditional monitoring methods.  **Significance**  By clarifying grant eligibility for sensor purchases, the bill may expand access to air quality monitoring tools for agencies with limited budgets, potentially improving data collection capabilities for air quality management and public health protection. The legislation serves a technical or clarifying function within existing Clean Air Act authorities rather than establishing new regulatory requirements or funding levels."
