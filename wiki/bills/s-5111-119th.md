summary: "# Bill Summary: Electronic Filing Identification Number Validation  This bill would require validation procedures for electronic filing identification (EFIN) numbers used to submit tax returns and related documents electronically. EFINs are unique identifiers assigned to tax professionals, firms, and organizations that file tax documents with the IRS on behalf of clients. The bill aims to establish safeguards to verify that EFINs are legitimate and belong to authorized users before they can be used for electronic filing.  The bill would primarily affect tax professionals, accounting firms, payroll processors, and other entities that electronically file tax documents with the IRS. It would also apply to the IRS, which would implement the validation procedures. By requiring validation, the measure seeks to prevent unauthorized use of EFINs and reduce identity theft, fraud, and the filing of false tax returns.  Key provisions would likely include requiring the IRS to develop and enforce validation procedures before accepting electronically filed documents and potentially establishing verification requirements for EFIN holders or applicants. The specific mechanics of validation—such as whether it involves multi-factor authentication, periodic re-verification, or other security measures—would depend on details in the full bill text."
