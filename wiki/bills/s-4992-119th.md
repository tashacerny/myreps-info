The guidance would affect parties seeking to modify or use hydropower projects, including private companies, utilities, and other entities operating these facilities. It would also apply to the Army Corps of Engineers, which oversees federal permitting and licensing processes for many water resource projects. By establishing clear guidance standards, the bill aims to create more consistent and transparent procedures for handling these applications across different projects and regions.
