summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill would amend the Social Security Act to limit how much of a beneficiary's monthly Social Security payment can be withheld to recover overpayments. Under the proposed change, no more than 10 percent of a monthly benefit could be withheld for this purpose in any given month.  **Who It Affects**  This legislation applies to Social Security beneficiaries who have received overpayments—instances where they received more benefits than they were entitled to receive. The Social Security Administration (SSA) currently has authority to recover these overpayments by withholding a portion of future monthly benefits. This bill would affect both current beneficiaries subject to ongoing recovery efforts and potentially the SSA's collection practices going forward.  **Key Provision**  The primary provision establishes a 10 percent monthly cap on benefit withholding for overpayment recovery. This means that even if a beneficiary owes a significant overpayment, only up to 10 percent of their monthly benefit amount could be withheld each month. This would extend the repayment timeline for overpayments but would ensure beneficiaries retain at least 90 percent of their monthly benefits during the recovery period."
