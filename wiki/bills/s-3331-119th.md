The facility would be officially designated as the \"2nd Lieutenant Mitch Williams Post Office.\" The bill affects the postal facility in Moab and the residents and businesses in that area who use the facility's services.  The bill's primary provision is a commemorative designation naming the postal facility after 2nd Lieutenant Mitch Williams. This type of legislation is typically used to honor individuals by attaching their name to a federal facility. The designation would be reflected in official postal records and signage at the location. No changes to the facility's operations or services are specified in the bill's title."
