summary: "# Bill Summary: Serbian American Heritage Month  This bill proposes to officially designate a month as \"Serbian American Heritage Month\" to recognize and celebrate the history, culture, and contributions of Serbian Americans to the United States. The designation would be a formal acknowledgment by Congress of Serbian American communities and their role in American society.  The bill affects Serbian American communities and organizations, as well as the general public through heightened awareness and cultural recognition. The designation would likely encourage educational activities, cultural events, and commemorations during the designated month. It would be primarily symbolic in nature, as such heritage month designations do not typically allocate funding or create new legal obligations, but rather serve to honor and promote awareness of a particular group's heritage and achievements.  Key provisions would include the official designation itself and potentially language encouraging federal agencies, schools, and communities to recognize and participate in celebrating Serbian American contributions to American history, culture, and society during the designated month."
