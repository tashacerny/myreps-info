summary: "# Summary of Bill: Withholding Senator Pay During Government Shutdown  This resolution proposes to withhold the salaries of U.S. Senators in the event of a federal government shutdown. Under the proposal, Senators would not receive their regular pay while a shutdown is in effect. The measure is designed to create a financial incentive for members of the Senate to reach agreement on government funding and avoid shutdowns.  **Who It Affects:** The resolution directly affects all sitting U.S. Senators. It does not apply to other federal employees, the House of Representatives, or the executive branch.  **Key Provisions:** The resolution would automatically suspend Senate pay during any period when federal government funding lapses and a shutdown occurs. Pay would presumably resume once government funding is restored. This type of measure attempts to align Senators' personal financial interests with resolving funding disputes quickly. Similar proposals have been introduced in various Congressional sessions, though they are separate from broader shutdown prevention legislation."
