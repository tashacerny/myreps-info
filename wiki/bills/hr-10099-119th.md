Currently, military personnel with relevant driving experience are eligible for CDL skills test waivers, but this eligibility is limited to a specific time window. The bill proposes to extend this window, making it easier for veterans and active-duty service members to obtain commercial driver licenses after their military service without retaking the skills test portion.  The bill affects military service members and veterans who have driving experience that qualifies them for CDL waivers under existing federal regulations. By extending the waiver period, the legislation aims to facilitate their transition to civilian employment, particularly in the transportation and trucking industries where CDL credentials are required.
