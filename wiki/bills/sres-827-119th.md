summary: "# Bill Summary  This resolution recognizes the 100th anniversary of the Sporting Arms and Ammunition Manufacturers' Institute, Inc. (SAAMI) and commends its work in establishing industry standards. SAAMI is a trade association composed of firearms and ammunition manufacturers. The resolution marks a centennial milestone for the organization's existence.  The bill highlights SAAMI's primary function: developing and maintaining technical standards that ensure firearms and ammunition are safe and compatible with one another. These standards address specifications such as pressure limits, dimensions, and performance metrics to promote interoperability across different manufacturers' products. By establishing uniform standards, SAAMI's work aims to reduce safety risks to end users and manufacturers alike.  The resolution does not create new laws or regulations; it is a commemorative measure expressing Congress's recognition of SAAMI's century-long role in the firearms and ammunition industry. It affects primarily the organization itself and its member manufacturers, as well as consumers who benefit from standardized, interoperable firearms and ammunition products."
