summary: "# Bill Summary  This joint resolution would disapprove of an Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) rule that granted California a waiver from federal clean air preemption standards. The waiver allows California to implement its Advanced Clean Car Program and Zero Emission Vehicle (ZEV) amendments for vehicles from 2017 and earlier model years. Under the Clean Air Act, California can request waivers to set its own vehicle emission standards that are stricter than federal standards, and other states can choose to adopt California's standards instead of federal ones.  The resolution uses the congressional disapproval process under the Administrative Procedure Act, which allows Congress to overturn agency rules within a specified timeframe. If passed, this resolution would invalidate the EPA's decision to grant California the waiver, effectively preventing California from enforcing its Advanced Clean Car Program and ZEV amendments under this particular waiver. The resolution would affect California's ability to regulate vehicle emissions within its borders and could impact automakers subject to California's standards, as well as other states that follow California's emission rules.  The key issue at stake is whether Congress agrees with the EPA's determination that California met the legal requirements for a Clean Air Act waiver. Supporters of the resolution would argue the waiver should not have been granted, while opponents would defend the EPA's authority to grant the waiver and California's right to set stricter emission standards."
