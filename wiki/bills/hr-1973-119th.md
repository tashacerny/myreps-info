summary: "# Summary: No Pay for Congress During Default or Shutdown Act  This bill proposes to withhold congressional salaries during federal government shutdowns or when the government reaches the debt ceiling without authorization to borrow additional funds (a default situation). The measure would suspend pay for members of Congress—both the House of Representatives and Senate—for the duration of any such fiscal crisis, resuming payments only when normal government operations are restored or debt ceiling authorization is secured.  The bill affects members of Congress, including representatives and senators from both parties. It is intended to create a financial incentive for lawmakers to resolve budget and debt-related disputes more quickly, based on the premise that legislators should face personal consequences when they cannot agree on appropriations or debt ceiling measures.  Key provisions would include: (1) automatic suspension of congressional salaries upon the onset of a shutdown or default; (2) resumption of pay once the shutdown ends or default is resolved; and (3) application to all voting members of both chambers. The bill does not appear to address whether back pay would be provided once operations resume, a detail that would affect the practical impact of the measure."
