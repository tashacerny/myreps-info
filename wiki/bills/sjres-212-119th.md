summary: "# Summary of Constitutional Amendment Bill on Voting Eligibility  This joint resolution proposes a constitutional amendment to establish that only U.S. citizens are eligible to vote in federal elections. Currently, the Constitution does not explicitly prohibit non-citizens from voting in federal elections, though federal law and all 50 states have implemented citizenship requirements for voter registration. This amendment would create an explicit constitutional standard on this matter.  The amendment would affect eligibility rules for voting in elections for federal offices, including the presidency, Senate, and House of Representatives. If approved by both chambers of Congress and ratified by three-fourths of state legislatures, it would become part of the Constitution and would supersede any conflicting state laws or regulations regarding federal election voting eligibility.  The key provision establishes citizenship as a constitutional requirement for voting in federal elections. The amendment does not address voting in state and local elections, which remain under state authority, nor does it modify other voting eligibility requirements such as age, residency, or registration status that are established elsewhere in constitutional or statutory law."
