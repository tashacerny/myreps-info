The report would inform lawmakers about the benefits, costs, and practical considerations of centralizing drone operations expertise and training within the Army National Guard rather than distributing these capabilities across different military branches or units.  The bill primarily affects the Army National Guard and the broader U.S. military structure. A Drone Center of Excellence, if created, would likely consolidate training, doctrine development, and technical expertise in unmanned aerial systems.
