summary: "# Summary: National Down Syndrome Awareness Month Designation  This bill expresses congressional support for designating October 2025 as \"National Down Syndrome Awareness Month.\" The measure is a symbolic resolution rather than legislation that creates binding requirements or programs. It acknowledges and promotes awareness of Down syndrome, a genetic condition caused by the presence of an extra chromosome 21, which affects approximately 1 in 700 births in the United States.  The designation would draw attention to Down syndrome and those living with the condition during the designated month. It may encourage educational activities, public health initiatives, and community engagement efforts focused on Down syndrome. The bill would affect advocacy organizations, medical professionals, educational institutions, and individuals and families affected by Down syndrome who may use the designation to coordinate awareness and support activities.  This type of resolution does not create funding, mandate specific programs, or establish legal requirements. Rather, it serves as a formal statement of congressional intent to recognize and support awareness efforts around the designated health condition and population during October 2025."
