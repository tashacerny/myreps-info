summary: "# Summary of Joint Resolution on U.S. Armed Forces in Iran  This joint resolution directs the removal of U.S. Armed Forces from hostilities involving Iran that lack congressional authorization. The resolution addresses military operations or conflicts with Iran that may occur without a formal declaration of war or congressional approval. It applies to the Department of Defense and any military personnel engaged in such hostilities.  The key provision requires the President to cease and remove U.S. Armed Forces from any ongoing or future military hostilities with Iran unless Congress has explicitly authorized such action through legislation. The resolution reflects the constitutional requirement that Congress, not the President alone, holds the power to declare war and authorize military force. The measure would establish a legal obligation to end any such unauthorized hostilities within a specified timeframe, though the exact implementation timeline would depend on the resolution's specific language.  This type of joint resolution is a legislative tool Congress uses to direct executive branch actions. If passed by both chambers and signed by the President (or passed over a presidential veto), it would carry the force of law."
