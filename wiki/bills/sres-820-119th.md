summary: "# Summary of National Lighthouse Day Resolution  This resolution designates August 7, 2026, as \"National Lighthouse Day\" in the United States. The measure is a commemorative resolution that establishes an official day to recognize and honor lighthouses and their historical significance to the nation. This type of resolution does not create new laws or mandate government action; instead, it serves as an official acknowledgment and encourages public awareness and observance of the designated day.  The resolution would affect the general public, lighthouse organizations, historical societies, and coastal communities that maintain or visit lighthouses. It provides an opportunity for these groups to organize educational events, celebrations, or commemorative activities. The specific provisions would likely include the designation of the date itself, and possibly a statement of purpose explaining the historical, cultural, or navigational importance of lighthouses to the United States.  As a commemorative resolution, this measure carries symbolic rather than substantive legislative weight and does not require appropriations or establish ongoing government programs or obligations."
