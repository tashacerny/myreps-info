summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill proposes to amend the Food Security Act of 1985 by adding the emergency watershed program to the list of covered programs under the regional conservation partnership program (RCPP). The RCPP is a federal initiative that partners the Department of Agriculture with non-federal entities—such as states, tribes, nonprofits, and private landowners—to address natural resource concerns at a regional level. By adding the emergency watershed program as a covered program, this bill would allow those two conservation efforts to work together more directly.  The emergency watershed program provides funding for projects that address watershed damage from natural disasters or degradation, such as flooding, erosion, or water quality issues. The change would affect landowners, conservation organizations, state and local governments, and tribal entities that participate in either program, potentially streamlining coordination and allowing combined funding or joint project planning between the two initiatives.  The specific mechanisms for how these programs would coordinate, what new funding or requirements would result, and other implementation details would depend on the full legislative text. The bill's phrase \"and for other purposes\" suggests it may contain additional provisions beyond the primary amendment to the Food Security Act."
