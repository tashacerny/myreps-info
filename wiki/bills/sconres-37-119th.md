summary: "# Bill Summary  This concurrent resolution would authorize the use of the Capitol rotunda for a ceremonial event honoring the late Senator Lindsey O. Graham. Concurrent resolutions are legislative measures that require approval from both the House and Senate but do not require presidential signature. The resolution addresses a procedural matter regarding the use of Capitol facilities rather than creating new law or policy.  The resolution would allow the Capitol rotunda—one of the most formal spaces in the legislative building—to be used for a memorial or tribute ceremony. This would affect the scheduling and use of the Capitol building and would involve both chambers of Congress in approving this ceremonial use. Such resolutions are typically introduced when Congress wishes to formally recognize a deceased member and conduct official remembrances in the Capitol building."
