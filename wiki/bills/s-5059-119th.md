summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill proposes to amend the Public Health Service Act to modify the regulatory review process for biosimilar biological products. Biosimilars are medications designed to be highly similar to already-approved biologic drugs, such as certain cancer treatments and immunosuppressive therapies. The bill aims to streamline the approval pathway for these products, likely by reducing bureaucratic steps, clarifying requirements, or accelerating timelines in the review process.  The changes would primarily affect pharmaceutical manufacturers seeking to bring biosimilar products to market, as well as patients and healthcare systems that use biologic medications. The FDA, which reviews and approves biosimilars under current law, would also be affected by any modifications to review procedures.  While the specific provisions are not detailed here, streamlining biosimilar reviews typically aims to reduce development costs and timelines for manufacturers, potentially increasing competition in the biologic drug market and expanding patient access to lower-cost alternatives to brand-name biologics."
