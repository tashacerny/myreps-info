summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill would authorize judicial review of decisions by the executive branch to terminate Temporary Protected Status (TPS) designations. TPS is a federal immigration program that allows foreign nationals from countries experiencing armed conflict, natural disasters, or other extraordinary conditions to remain and work in the United States on a temporary basis. The bill addresses the legal process by which the Secretary of Homeland Security can end TPS for a country, making such termination decisions subject to court review rather than being final administrative determinations.  The bill would primarily affect individuals currently holding TPS and potentially the countries whose TPS designations are under review. It would also impact the Department of Homeland Security's authority to manage the program. By requiring judicial review, the bill would allow affected TPS holders or other parties to challenge termination decisions in federal court, likely on grounds such as whether the termination decision followed proper legal procedures or was supported by adequate factual evidence regarding conditions in the affected country.  The practical effect would be to add a judicial check on executive branch decisions about TPS, potentially delaying terminations pending court decisions and providing a forum for legal challenge to such actions."
