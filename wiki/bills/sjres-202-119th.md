summary: "# Summary of Congressional Disapproval Resolution  **What the Bill Proposes**  This joint resolution seeks to disapprove a Small Business Administration (SBA) rule under the Congressional Review Act (a procedure that allows Congress to overturn recent federal regulations). The rule being challenged concerns citizenship and residency requirements for applicants seeking SBA-backed loans, specifically the 7(a) and 504 loan programs. The resolution would block the rule from taking effect if passed by both chambers and signed by the President.  **Who It Affects**  The rule primarily affects individuals and entities applying for SBA-backed business loans. It would also impact the SBA, which would be required to maintain previous requirements rather than implement the new standards. Small business owners, loan applicants, and lending institutions that participate in these SBA programs could be affected depending on how the citizenship and residency requirements change between the old and new rules.  **Key Provisions**  The resolution targets two related SBA actions: the rescission of Procedural Notice 5000-872050 and the implementation of revised applicant ownership, citizenship, and residency requirements. The exact nature of how requirements would change is not specified in the title alone, but the resolution would prevent the new rule from becoming effective if approved by Congress."
