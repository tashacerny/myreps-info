summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill would require the Secretary of the Interior to conduct meaningful public engagement and consult with state, tribal, and local government officials before making decisions about whether to offer federal land parcels for oil and gas leasing. The requirement applies to certain parcels designated for potential leasing. The bill aims to ensure that affected communities and governments have an opportunity to participate in the decision-making process before leasing determinations are finalized.  The bill would primarily affect the Department of the Interior's oil and gas leasing program and the federal lands subject to leasing decisions. It impacts state and local governments, tribal nations, and members of the public with interests in federal land use decisions. Oil and gas companies pursuing leasing opportunities on federal lands would also be affected by any changes to the timing or process of leasing decisions.  Key provisions would likely include requirements for the Secretary to notify and consult with state, tribal, and local officials before making leasing decisions, establish timelines for public comment periods, and document how public input was considered. The bill does not appear to restrict oil and gas leasing itself, but rather mandates a more inclusive procedural process prior to leasing decisions."
