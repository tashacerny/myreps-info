summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill is a congressional disapproval resolution under the Congressional Review Act (CRA), a legislative process that allows Congress to overturn federal regulations. It targets a specific rule issued by the Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) concerning California's nonroad engine pollution control standards and commercial harbor craft regulations. The rule in question relates to the EPA's decision regarding California's authority to set its own emissions standards for these sources—a power California has historically held under the Clean Air Act.  The bill would affect the EPA's regulatory authority, commercial harbor craft operators (such as tugboats and ferries), engine manufacturers, and potentially California's ability to enforce stricter emissions standards than federal requirements. If passed, the resolution would nullify the EPA rule and prevent the agency from issuing substantially the same rule without new congressional authorization.  Key provisions typical of CRA disapprovals include: the rule would be deemed \"disapproved,\" effectively overturning it; the EPA would be prohibited from reissuing the same rule; and the action would generally need to pass both chambers of Congress and be signed by the President to take effect. The specific impacts depend on what decision the EPA made in the underlying rule regarding California's regulatory authority."
