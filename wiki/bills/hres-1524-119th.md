summary: "# Summary: National Estuaries Week Designation (2026)  This resolution expresses Congressional support for designating the week of September 19–26, 2026, as \"National Estuaries Week.\" The measure is a statement of support rather than legislation that creates new laws or funding. It aims to raise public awareness about estuaries—coastal areas where rivers meet oceans and freshwater mixes with saltwater—and their ecological and economic importance.  The designation would affect federal, state, and local governments, educational institutions, and nonprofit organizations that may use the week to promote estuaries through educational events, conservation activities, and public outreach. No specific groups are targeted for regulation or restriction.  Key aspects include the formal Congressional recognition of the designated week and the implicit encouragement for stakeholders to use this timeframe for estuaries-related activities and messaging. Since this is a commemorative resolution rather than substantive legislation, it carries symbolic rather than binding legal authority."
