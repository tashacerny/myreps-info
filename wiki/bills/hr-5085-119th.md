summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill would create an exemption from the National Environmental Policy Act (NEPA) for certain federal actions related to infill housing construction. NEPA currently requires federal agencies to prepare environmental assessments or environmental impact statements before taking major actions that may significantly affect the environment. The bill proposes to waive these requirements for infill housing projects—typically housing built on undeveloped or previously developed land within existing urban or developed areas.  The exemption would apply to federal actions that support infill housing, which could include federal funding, permits, or approvals for such projects. This would primarily affect federal agencies overseeing housing programs, environmental reviews, and land-use decisions. Infill housing developers and communities seeking to increase housing density in existing developed areas could benefit from streamlined federal approval processes. Environmental review requirements for these projects would no longer apply at the federal level.  The stated purpose is to expedite federal involvement in infill housing development by removing procedural requirements. Supporters of such exemptions typically argue it reduces project timelines and costs, while critics generally contend that exempting projects from environmental review may bypass important safeguards. The bill's scope depends on how \"infill housing\" and \"federal actions\" are defined in the full text."
