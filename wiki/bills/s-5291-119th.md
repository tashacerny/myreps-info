summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill would require the Comptroller General (the head of the Government Accountability Office, a nonpartisan congressional agency) to conduct an evaluation of how the federal government currently provides cybersecurity assistance to small business concerns. The evaluation would examine existing federal cybersecurity programs and support services available to small businesses, assess their effectiveness, and likely identify gaps or areas for improvement in how these resources are delivered.  The bill would primarily affect small businesses seeking cybersecurity support and federal agencies that administer cybersecurity assistance programs. The Comptroller General would be tasked with reviewing which federal agencies offer cybersecurity help to small businesses, what types of assistance are available (such as training, guidance, vulnerability assessments, or funding), and how effectively these programs are reaching and serving small business owners.  By requiring this evaluation, the bill aims to provide Congress with factual information about the current landscape of federal cybersecurity assistance to small businesses. This information could inform future legislative decisions about whether additional support is needed, whether existing programs should be expanded or modified, or how to better coordinate cybersecurity resources across federal agencies."
