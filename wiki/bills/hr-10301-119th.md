summary: "# Bill Summary: Congressional Gold Medal for Peter McGuire  This bill proposes to award a Congressional Gold Medal to Peter McGuire in recognition of his contributions to the American labor movement. The Congressional Gold Medal is one of the highest civilian honors Congress can bestow and is typically awarded to individuals who have made outstanding contributions to American society or culture.  Peter McGuire was a prominent labor organizer and activist in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. He is historically credited with founding the American Federation of Labor (AFL) alongside Samuel Gompers and is recognized as a key figure in establishing Labor Day as a national holiday. The bill honors his work advocating for workers' rights, labor organization, and workplace improvements during a formative period of American labor history.  If enacted, this bill would authorize the minting and presentation of a gold medal bearing McGuire's likeness and accomplishments. The medal would serve as an official congressional recognition of his historical legacy. This action affects no specific current policies or programs but represents a ceremonial acknowledgment of McGuire's historical significance to American labor and workers' rights."
