summary: "# Withhold Member Pay During Shutdowns Act  **Summary**  This bill would require that members of Congress forfeit their salaries during any period when the federal government is shut down. A government shutdown occurs when Congress fails to pass appropriations legislation or a continuing resolution before the current funding authority expires, causing most federal operations and spending to halt. The bill aims to create a direct financial consequence for lawmakers during shutdowns.  **Who It Affects**  The legislation applies to all voting members of the U.S. House of Representatives and Senate. It does not affect federal employees, contractors, or other government personnel, though shutdown rules generally apply to them separately through existing law.  **Key Provisions**  The bill would withhold pay for members during shutdown periods, with compensation resuming once appropriations or a continuing resolution is enacted and normal operations resume. The specific mechanics—such as whether withheld pay is recovered after a shutdown ends or permanently forfeited, and how partial-month shutdowns are calculated—would depend on the bill's detailed language. This type of proposal reflects the reasoning that members should share directly in the consequences of shutdown-related gridlock."
