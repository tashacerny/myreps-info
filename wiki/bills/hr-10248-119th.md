summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill proposes amendments to the Immigration and Nationality Act to create new visa pathways for individuals in public service roles. Specifically, it would establish a new class of nonimmigrant visas for public service employees, allowing certain workers in public service positions to obtain temporary legal status. Additionally, the bill would authorize lawful permanent resident status (green cards) for certain individuals who are currently in public service jobs and entered the United States as children.  The bill primarily affects two groups: foreign nationals seeking to work in public service positions in the United States, and individuals who arrived in the country as children and are now employed in public service roles. Public service employees could include workers in education, healthcare, law enforcement, emergency services, social services, and similar fields, though the specific definitions would be determined by the bill's provisions.  Without access to the full text, the exact details of eligibility requirements, visa caps, application procedures, and which public service sectors qualify cannot be specified.
