The bill affects two main groups: (1) persons who refuse to answer questions when called before Congress, and (2) persons who are finally convicted of perjury during congressional testimony or proceedings. These individuals would become ineligible for federal employment as a consequence of their actions or convictions.  Key provisions include establishing a debarment mechanism that would prevent covered individuals from working for any federal agency or department.
