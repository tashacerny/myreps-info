summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill would amend the federal tax code to eliminate penalties associated with the sale and use of dyed fuel in taxable contexts. Dyed fuel is diesel or gasoline that has been marked with dye to indicate it is intended for non-taxable uses, such as agriculture, heating, or off-road equipment. The bill would remove the financial penalties currently imposed when dyed fuel is sold for or used in taxable applications—contexts where the fuel tax normally applies, such as fueling vehicles on public roads.  The bill would affect fuel distributors, retailers, and end-users who currently face penalties for misuse or misdistribution of dyed fuel. It would also impact federal fuel tax revenue, as dyed fuel avoids the standard excise tax, and expanding its permissible uses could reduce tax collection.
