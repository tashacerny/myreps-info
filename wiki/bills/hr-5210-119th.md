summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill proposes technical amendments to federal law that would update outdated references and correct errors in three major sections of the U.S. Code: Title 2 (Congress and legislative matters), Title 50 (War and National Defense), and Title 52 (Voting and Elections). These changes are administrative in nature, intended to ensure that statutory cross-references point to the correct current locations of laws rather than repealed or relocated provisions.  The bill affects all federal agencies, legislative bodies, and entities that work with these titles of law, including congressional offices, election administrators, and national defense agencies. When laws are reorganized or repealed, references to them in other statutes can become outdated or inaccurate, which can create confusion and administrative inefficiencies.  The specific provisions would correct these outdated references and related technical errors throughout the U.S. Code. These types of technical correction bills are routine legislative housekeeping measures designed to maintain the accuracy and clarity of federal law without substantively changing policy or existing legal obligations. They ensure that references within the legal code remain consistent and functional for government operations and public access to the law."
