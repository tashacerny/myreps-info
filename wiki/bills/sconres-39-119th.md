summary: "# Summary of Congressional Budget Resolution for Fiscal Year 2027  This concurrent resolution establishes the federal government's overall spending and revenue framework for fiscal year 2027 and provides budgetary targets through 2036. As a budget resolution, this measure does not directly appropriate funds or change tax law; instead, it sets aggregate spending levels and establishes guidelines that Congress will use when drafting specific appropriations bills and tax legislation. The resolution affects all federal agencies and programs, as well as taxpayers, beneficiaries of federal programs, and recipients of federal funding.  Key provisions typically found in such resolutions include total spending caps for discretionary spending (annual appropriations), targets for mandatory spending (entitlement programs), revenue projections, and deficit or surplus estimates. The resolution also establishes reconciliation instructions if Congress intends to use the reconciliation process—a procedural tool that allows certain bills to pass the Senate with a simple majority rather than a 60-vote supermajority. The 10-year window (2027–2036) allows Congress to demonstrate the long-term fiscal effects of policy decisions.  This resolution serves as a blueprint for subsequent legislative action. Individual committees use these targets when developing spending bills and tax provisions. The resolution itself requires passage by both chambers but does not require the President's signature."
