summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill would prohibit the purchase and service of seafood products originating from China in all congressional dining facilities, including cafeterias, restaurants, and catering services that operate within the Capitol building and House and Senate office buildings. The restriction would apply to all seafood regardless of processing location, meaning products caught or farmed in China would be excluded even if processed elsewhere. The bill affects the operations of congressional food service providers and the procurement decisions made for feeding members of Congress, their staff, and visitors.  The bill's key provision is a straightforward purchasing ban that would require congressional food service operations to source seafood from non-Chinese suppliers. This would necessitate changes to vendor contracts and supply chain management. The bill title indicates there may be additional provisions (\"and for other purposes\"), though without access to the full text, these cannot be detailed. The practical effect would be to increase compliance and verification requirements for food service providers to ensure seafood origins meet the specified criteria."
