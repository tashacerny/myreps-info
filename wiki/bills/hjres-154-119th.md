summary: "# Summary of Congressional Disapproval of DOL Adverse Effect Wage Rate Rule  This bill is a resolution of disapproval under the Congressional Review Act (CRA), which allows Congress to overturn federal agency rules. Specifically, it seeks to reject a rule issued by the Department of Labor regarding the Adverse Effect Wage Rate (AEWR). The AEWR is the minimum wage that employers must pay foreign workers admitted under the H-2A temporary agricultural visa program, designed to prevent those workers from undercutting wages for U.S. workers in the same positions.  If passed, this resolution would nullify the Department of Labor's rule on the AEWR. The affected parties include agricultural employers who hire temporary foreign workers under H-2A visas, foreign workers in those programs, and domestic agricultural workers whose wages the rule is intended to protect. The specific changes the Department of Labor proposed are not detailed in the title alone, but disapproving the rule would mean the prior AEWR standard would remain in effect or a new rulemaking process would be required.  This type of resolution requires passage by both chambers of Congress and the president's signature to take effect. If successful, it would also prevent the Department of Labor from issuing a substantially similar rule without new congressional authorization."
