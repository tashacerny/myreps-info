The primary beneficiaries would be law enforcement agencies and officers in rural and smaller communities that currently have limited access to training programs. By using grants to support nonprofit providers, the bill aims to expand training capacity in underserved areas.  Key provisions would likely include establishing a grant program within DOJ, defining eligibility criteria for nonprofit applicants and law enforcement recipients, and authorizing funding levels for the initiative. The bill would address a potential gap in law enforcement professional development for smaller jurisdictions that may lack the resources or infrastructure to conduct specialized training internally or send officers to distant training centers."
