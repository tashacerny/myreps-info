summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill proposes to amend federal law to establish recusal requirements for executive branch officers and employees when they have matters before them that could affect the financial interests of their previous employers. In other words, it would require federal employees to step aside from decisions, cases, or other official actions involving companies or organizations where they previously worked, if those decisions could financially impact their former employer.  The bill would apply to officials and staff throughout the executive branch—including agency heads, appointees, and career employees. It aims to prevent potential conflicts of interest that could arise when federal officials make decisions affecting entities where they have recent employment history. The bill's scope and specific standards for determining when recusal is required would depend on the detailed provisions in the full legislative text.  The stated intent is to promote ethical governance by reducing the appearance or reality of conflicts of interest within the federal government. This type of measure affects the conduct of executive branch operations and the management of federal employee ethics standards."
