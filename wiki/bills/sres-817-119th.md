summary: "# Bill Summary  This resolution authorizes the Senate to consider multiple executive nominations together (known as \"en bloc consideration\") during a closed executive session rather than through individual consideration. En bloc procedures allow the Senate to vote on several nominations simultaneously, which streamlines the confirmation process for nominations that do not require individual debate or separate votes.  The resolution affects nominees awaiting Senate confirmation across various executive branch positions and agencies. By allowing multiple nominations to be considered together in executive session, the measure is designed to expedite the confirmation timeline for these candidates while maintaining the Senate's oversight function.  The key provision enables en bloc consideration, meaning the Senate can approve several nominations with a single vote during executive session, rather than conducting separate votes for each nominee. This procedural tool is commonly used for nominations expected to pass without significant controversy, allowing the Senate to manage its calendar more efficiently while still preserving the ability to pull individual nominations from the en bloc group for separate consideration if needed."
