summary: "# Summary  This resolution condemns a terrorist bombing that occurred at the Argentine Jewish Mutual Association (AMIA) Jewish Community Center in Buenos Aires on July 18, 1994. The attack killed 85 people and injured over 300 others, making it one of the deadliest attacks on a Jewish institution in the Western Hemisphere. The resolution expresses disapproval of the attack and calls for accountability for those responsible.  The resolution primarily affects the congressional record and U.S. diplomatic messaging, as it represents an official statement by Congress on the historical attack. It may also have implications for U.S. relations with Argentina regarding ongoing investigations and prosecutions related to the bombing. The resolution encourages pursuit of justice for victims and their families, though it does not authorize specific government action or appropriations."
