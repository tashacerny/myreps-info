summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill directs the U.S. Secretary of Education to conduct a study examining how states, school districts, and public schools currently teach students about the September 11, 2001 terrorist attacks. The study would assess educational efforts across the country to understand what content is being covered, how it is being taught, and the extent to which 9/11 education is integrated into school curricula.  The bill affects the Department of Education, which would be responsible for carrying out the research, as well as state education agencies, local school districts, and public elementary and secondary schools, whose 9/11 education practices would be examined. The study would provide a comprehensive overview of current approaches to this historical topic in American education.  Key provisions would likely include gathering data on curriculum materials, instructional methods, and student learning outcomes related to 9/11 education. The study would provide policymakers and educators with information about existing practices, which could inform future decisions about how this significant historical event is taught in American schools."
