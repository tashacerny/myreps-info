The conveyance represents a transfer of federal property to state control for the specific purpose of providing housing and care services to veterans.  The bill affects the Department of the Army, the State of North Carolina, and veterans who may use the facility. It involves the disposition of federal real estate and the establishment of a state-operated veterans' facility. Key provisions would include the Secretary of the Army's authority to execute the property transfer and likely specify conditions on how the property must be used and maintained by the state."
