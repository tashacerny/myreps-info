Specifically, it would extend educational benefits to dependent parents of veterans who either die from a service-connected disability or have a total and permanent service-connected disability. Currently, such educational assistance is primarily available to spouses and children of eligible veterans; this legislation would add dependent parents as a new category of beneficiaries.  The bill would affect dependent parents of veterans meeting the specified disability criteria. These parents would become eligible to receive educational assistance benefits, allowing them to pursue educational and training programs. The expansion represents a change in how the Department of Veterans Affairs structures its survivor and dependent assistance programs, as it adds a new family relationship category to the existing benefit structure.  The phrase \"and for other purposes\" in the bill's title suggests it may contain additional provisions beyond the main dependent parent eligibility change, though without the full text, those cannot be detailed here.
