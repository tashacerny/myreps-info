summary: "# Bill Summary  This bill would establish an oral history program within the Library of Congress to collect and preserve firsthand accounts related to the September 11, 2001 terrorist attacks. The program would systematically document personal testimonies from individuals with direct experience of the attacks, their immediate aftermath, and response efforts.  The program would likely affect survivors, first responders (such as firefighters, police officers, and emergency medical personnel), family members of victims, and other individuals connected to the events of September 11. The Library of Congress would oversee collection, preservation, and public access to these recorded histories as part of the nation's historical record.  Key provisions would typically include authorization for the Library of Congress to conduct interviews, establish standards for recording and archiving oral histories, and determine access policies for the collected materials. The program would serve both historical documentation and educational purposes, making these accounts available to researchers and the public for years to come."
