Eviction diversion programs are designed to help keep renters in their homes by offering alternatives to formal eviction proceedings, such as mediation between landlords and tenants, rental assistance, or legal aid. The grant program would apply to units of general local government, meaning counties, cities, and other local jurisdictions.  The primary beneficiaries would include tenants at risk of eviction, landlords who may participate in diversion programs, and local governments seeking to reduce eviction filings and their associated costs. By funding these programs at the local level, the bill aims to provide communities with resources to address housing instability and keep people from losing their housing.
